## Section 1 — Module Overview

### 1.1 Business Purpose

The Cafeteria module digitises the complete running of a school's canteen and hostel mess. It lets a school plan and publish meals with full nutrition information, take advance meal orders from students and parents, run a cashless prepaid meal wallet, give the kitchen an exact head-count for cooking, track raw-material stock, and keep food-safety (FSSAI) compliance records. Without it, schools manage menus on a whiteboard, take cash at the counter, cook by guesswork (causing waste and shortages), and have no record of which child ate what — a real risk for children with allergies.

### 1.2 Business Value

- **Eliminates cash handling at the counter** through a prepaid meal card, reducing theft, loss, and reconciliation effort.
- **Cuts food wastage and shortages** by giving the kitchen an exact, item-wise preparation count from advance orders and subscriptions.
- **Protects children with dietary needs** by surfacing allergy and preference flags at the ordering screen and the serving counter.
- **Gives parents transparency** — they can see the menu, top up the wallet, order on their child's behalf, and view what their child ate.
- **Keeps the school inspection-ready** with a permanent FSSAI licence and hygiene-audit log and timely licence-expiry reminders.
- **Improves accountability** with a complete, tamper-proof money trail for every credit, debit, and refund on a meal wallet.

### 1.3 Scope

#### In Scope
1. Meal-type categories (Breakfast, Lunch, Snacks, Dinner, Tuck Shop) and a dish library with price, food type, nutrition, and allergen notes.
2. Weekly menu planning on a day-by-meal grid, with a draft/publish/archive lifecycle and parent/student notification on publish.
3. Special and festival/event meals, optionally limited to chosen classes.
4. Per-student dietary profile (preference, allergy and medical-diet flags).
5. Fixed meal subscription plans (monthly/quarterly/termly/annual) and student/staff enrolment, including hostel-mess auto-enrolment.
6. Advance meal ordering by students and parents with an enforced ordering cut-off and immediate confirmation.
7. A consolidated kitchen preparation view with dietary breakdowns and a printable kitchen sheet.
8. A touch-friendly counter (point-of-sale) interface for walk-in purchases with QR student lookup and dietary checks.
9. QR-based meal attendance capture (who actually ate) at the serving counter.
10. A prepaid meal wallet per student with online (Razorpay) and cash top-up, atomic balance deduction, and a complete transaction ledger.
11. Raw-material stock register with reorder alerts, daily consumption logging, and optional purchase-request hand-off to Inventory.
12. Cafeteria supplier register with FSSAI licence tracking.
13. School FSSAI licence and hygiene-audit log with expiry reminders.
14. Separate staff meal logging with a payroll-deduction signal.
15. A cafeteria dashboard and a set of operational and financial reports (revenue, daily sales, order summary, wastage, wallet statements, attendance, FSSAI).

#### Out of Scope
1. Online payment gateway configuration and settlement — handled by the **Payment** module (Razorpay setup) and its shared configuration.
2. Salary deduction for staff meals — the Cafeteria module only raises a deduction signal; the actual deduction is performed by the **Payroll (HrStaff)** module.
3. Student master data (names, classes, sections, photographs) — owned by the **StudentProfile** module; the Cafeteria only references it.
4. Academic year and term definitions — owned by **SchoolSetup**.
5. General school procurement and purchase orders — owned by the **Inventory** and **Vendor** modules; the Cafeteria only hands off a purchase request.
6. Message/notification delivery (push, SMS, email) — composed and dispatched by the **Notification** module.
7. Hostel admission and room allocation — owned by the **Hostel** module, which triggers cafeteria mess enrolment.

### 1.4 Key Terminology

| Business Term | Meaning |
|---------------|---------|
| Meal Card (Wallet) | A prepaid canteen wallet linked to one student; balance is deducted when an order is placed or a counter sale is made. |
| Meal Category | A named meal slot in the day — Breakfast, Lunch, Snacks, Dinner, or Tuck Shop. |
| Menu Item (Dish) | A dish in the master library, with price, food type (Veg/Non-Veg/Egg/Jain), nutrition, and allergen notes. |
| Weekly Menu | A set of daily menus for a Monday–Sunday week; one menu record per calendar date. |
| Event Meal | A one-off special menu for a festival, sports day, or excursion, optionally limited to selected classes. |
| Subscription Plan | A fixed-price plan (e.g. "Full Day — Breakfast + Lunch + Snacks") that auto-confirms a student's meals without daily ordering. |
| Hostel Mess Plan | A subscription plan automatically assigned to hostel residents when they are admitted to the hostel. |
| Order Cut-off | A configurable time before a meal is served, after which advance ordering for that meal closes. |
| Kitchen View | A consolidated preparation list showing item totals and dietary breakdowns for a chosen date and meal. |
| Meal Attendance | A record of which students actually received a meal, captured by scanning a QR code at the counter. |
| Point-of-Sale (Counter) Session | A counter staff member's shift during which walk-in purchases are processed and later reconciled. |
| Dietary Profile | A per-student record of food preference and allergy/restriction flags. |
| Reorder Level | The minimum stock quantity that, when reached, triggers a purchase alert. |
| Wastage | The gap between meals planned (from orders) and meals actually served (from attendance/consumption). |
| FSSAI | Food Safety and Standards Authority of India — the body governing food-safety compliance for school canteens. |

## Section 3 — Functional Requirements

### 3.1 Menu Category Management
**Requirement ID:** REQ-CAF-001
**Priority:** Core (P0)
**Category Tags:** [DATA_ENTRY] [CONFIGURATION]

#### Business Description
The school maintains the meal-type categories that organise everything else in the cafeteria — Breakfast, Lunch, Snacks, Dinner, and Tuck Shop. Each category carries a scheduled serving start time (used to calculate the ordering cut-off) and a display order that controls how it appears on the ordering screen. The Cafeteria Manager creates and maintains these categories; they are the foundation on which dishes, weekly menus, and orders are built.

#### Actors
- **Initiates:** School Admin, Cafeteria Manager
- **Processes / Approves:** School Admin, Cafeteria Manager
- **Views / Receives notification:** Students and Parents (indirectly, via ordering screens)

#### Business Rules
| Rule ID | Business Rule | Rule Type |
|---------|--------------|-----------|
| BR-CAF-025 | A category cannot be permanently deleted while any dish is still linked to it; it may only be deactivated. | Validation |
| BR-CAF-026 | The serving start time on a category drives the ordering cut-off calculation for every meal of that category. | Calculation |

#### Acceptance Criteria
1. A manager can create, edit, deactivate/reactivate, and archive a meal category, and restore an archived one.
2. The display order set on a category controls the sort sequence of meals on the ordering screen.
3. An attempt to permanently delete a category that still has dishes attached is blocked with a clear message.
4. Each category's serving start time is recorded and used when calculating ordering cut-off times.

#### Integration with Other Modules
- Receives from: None
- Sends to: None (consumed internally by menu, ordering, and attendance features)

#### Enhancement Notes (Future)
None.

### 3.2 Menu Item (Dish) Master
**Requirement ID:** REQ-CAF-002
**Priority:** Core (P0)
**Category Tags:** [DATA_ENTRY] [CONFIGURATION]

#### Business Description
The school maintains a dish library — every item that can appear on a menu — with its price, food type (Veg, Non-Veg, Egg, Jain), nutrition values (calories, protein, carbohydrate, fat), free-text allergen notes, and an optional photo. Each dish can be switched "available" or "sold out" in real time without removing it from a published menu, so the kitchen can mark a dish unavailable mid-day.

#### Actors
- **Initiates:** School Admin, Cafeteria Manager
- **Processes / Approves:** School Admin, Cafeteria Manager
- **Views / Receives notification:** Students, Parents, Kitchen Staff (on ordering and kitchen screens)

#### Business Rules
| Rule ID | Business Rule | Rule Type |
|---------|--------------|-----------|
| BR-CAF-027 | A dish marked "sold out" is hidden from the counter selection and shown greyed-out on the ordering screen, but remains on the published menu. | Workflow |
| BR-CAF-028 | A dish price must be a positive amount. | Validation |

#### Acceptance Criteria
1. A manager can create and edit a dish with price, food type, nutrition values, allergen notes, and a photo.
2. Jain and Veg dishes display a distinguishing badge on the ordering screen.
3. Toggling a dish's availability takes effect immediately, without removing it from the weekly plan.
4. Nutrition values are optional but, when entered, are shown on the ordering screen.
5. A dish photo can be uploaded and a thumbnail appears on the kitchen and ordering screens.

#### Integration with Other Modules
- Receives from: System media storage — dish photographs
- Sends to: None

#### Enhancement Notes (Future)
Standardised allergen icons (see ENH-CAF-004) and item barcodes for counter scanning (see ENH-CAF-009).

### 3.3 Weekly Menu Planning & Publish
**Requirement ID:** REQ-CAF-003
**Priority:** Core (P0)
**Category Tags:** [DATA_ENTRY] [WORKFLOW] [NOTIFICATION] [APPROVAL]

#### Business Description
The Cafeteria Manager plans a full week by placing dishes into day-by-meal slots on a grid. The plan starts as a draft; when it is published it becomes visible on the student and parent portals and a notification is sent to all active students and parents. Each calendar date has exactly one menu record, so editing a day updates that record rather than creating duplicates. Once a week passes, its menu is archived but remains readable for history.

#### Actors
- **Initiates:** Cafeteria Manager
- **Processes / Approves:** School Admin, Cafeteria Manager (publish)
- **Views / Receives notification:** Students, Parents

#### Business Rules
| Rule ID | Business Rule | Rule Type |
|---------|--------------|-----------|
| BR-CAF-005 | A weekly menu may be published only if at least one dish is assigned to at least one day-and-meal slot. | Workflow |
| BR-CAF-006 | Publishing a weekly menu sends a notification to all active students and parents. | Workflow |
| BR-CAF-018 | Only one menu record may exist per calendar date. | Validation |

#### Acceptance Criteria
1. The planning grid covers Monday–Sunday across all active meal categories.
2. The same dish may appear on multiple days but cannot be placed twice in the same day-and-meal slot.
3. Publishing is blocked, with a clear message, when no dishes are assigned to any slot.
4. On publish, the menu's status becomes Published, the publish time is recorded, and a notification is sent to active students and parents.
5. A published menu is visible as a day-wise grid on the student and parent portals.
6. Archived menus remain readable but do not appear on the active ordering screen.

#### Integration with Other Modules
- Receives from: SchoolSetup — academic term context
- Sends to: Notification — publish announcement; Student Portal and Parent Portal — published menu

#### Enhancement Notes (Future)
Smart menu rotation suggestions (see ENH-CAF-002) and a public menu photo gallery (see ENH-CAF-007).

### 3.5 Student Dietary Profile
**Requirement ID:** REQ-CAF-005
**Priority:** Core (P0)
**Category Tags:** [DATA_ENTRY]

#### Business Description
Each student has one dietary profile recording food preference, specific restrictions (no onion-garlic, gluten-free, nut allergy, dairy-free), and confidential medical-diet notes. These flags appear at the counter on scan, on the kitchen preparation list, and as warnings when a conflicting dish is ordered. Parents can maintain their child's profile through the Parent Portal, and every change is logged.

#### Actors
- **Initiates:** School Admin, Cafeteria Manager, Parent (for own child)
- **Processes / Approves:** School Admin, Cafeteria Manager
- **Views / Receives notification:** Kitchen Staff, Counter Staff

#### Business Rules
| Rule ID | Business Rule | Rule Type |
|---------|--------------|-----------|
| BR-CAF-002 | When a student whose profile is Jain or nut-allergic orders a conflicting dish, a dietary-conflict warning is shown; an admin may override, a student may not. | Validation |
| BR-CAF-029 | Each student may have only one dietary profile; updating an existing student replaces (upserts) their profile rather than creating a second one. | Validation |

#### Acceptance Criteria
1. Saving a profile for a student who already has one updates the existing profile (one profile per student).
2. A parent can update their child's profile through the portal and the change is recorded with who changed it.
3. On a counter scan or in the kitchen view, dietary flags display prominently as coloured chips.
4. A dietary-conflict warning is shown when a Jain or nut-allergic student orders a conflicting dish.
5. Confidential medical-diet notes are visible to kitchen staff but not to other students.

#### Integration with Other Modules

### 3.6 Meal Subscription Plans & Enrolment
**Requirement ID:** REQ-CAF-006
**Priority:** Standard (P1)
**Category Tags:** [CONFIGURATION] [DATA_ENTRY] [WORKFLOW] [INTEGRATION]

#### Business Description
The school offers fixed meal plans (for example, "Full Day Plan — Breakfast + Lunch + Snacks — ₹2,500/month"). A plan defines which meal categories it covers, its price, its billing period (monthly, quarterly, termly, or annual), and the academic term it belongs to. Students (or staff) are enrolled in a plan linked to their meal wallet, and the plan amount is deducted from the wallet at enrolment. Enrolled students are automatically counted as confirmed for their plan's meals each day without placing individual orders. Hostel residents are auto-enrolled in the hostel mess plan when they are admitted to the hostel.

#### Actors
- **Initiates:** School Admin (plans and enrolment); Hostel module (auto-enrolment)
- **Processes / Approves:** School Admin (early-exit refund)
- **Views / Receives notification:** Cafeteria Manager, Kitchen Staff, Hostel Warden, Parent

#### Business Rules
| Rule ID | Business Rule | Rule Type |
|---------|--------------|-----------|
| BR-CAF-010 | Subscription-enrolled students are pre-counted in the kitchen head-count for their plan's meals even without an explicit order record. | Calculation |
| BR-CAF-015 | When a student is admitted to the hostel, a hostel-mess enrolment is created automatically and the plan price is deducted from the student's meal wallet. | Workflow |
| BR-CAF-030 | Ending a plan early triggers a pro-rata refund proportional to the unused period, requiring manual admin approval. | Calculation |

#### Acceptance Criteria
1. A plan defines its included meal categories, price, billing period, and academic term.
2. Enrolling a student links the enrolment to a meal wallet and deducts the plan amount.
3. Enrolled students appear pre-confirmed in the kitchen view for their included meals.
4. A hostel resident is auto-enrolled in the hostel mess plan upon hostel admission.
5. An early exit produces a pro-rata refund figure that requires admin approval before it is applied.
6. An enrolment report lists active enrolments by plan, class, and section.

#### Integration with Other Modules
- Receives from: SchoolSetup — academic term; Hostel — admission trigger; StudentProfile — student identity
- Sends to: Meal wallet — plan-price deduction

#### Enhancement Notes (Future)
None.

### 3.7 Meal Pre-Ordering (Portal)
**Requirement ID:** REQ-CAF-007
**Priority:** Core (P0)
**Category Tags:** [DATA_ENTRY] [WORKFLOW] [NOTIFICATION] [INTEGRATION]

#### Business Description
Students and parents browse the published menu and book meals in advance for specific days and meal categories. Orders are confirmed immediately on placement; the cost is deducted from the prepaid meal wallet (or marked pay-at-counter). Ordering closes a configurable number of hours before the meal is served. Orders can be cancelled before the cut-off, with an immediate wallet refund. Each order carries a unique, formatted order number. Orders are placed through the portal and counter, not created manually by an administrator on the web screen, which is used to view and manage orders.

#### Actors
- **Initiates:** Student, Parent
- **Processes / Approves:** System (cut-off and balance checks); Cafeteria Manager (status updates)
- **Views / Receives notification:** Parent (order confirmation), Kitchen Staff

#### Business Rules
| Rule ID | Business Rule | Rule Type |
|---------|--------------|-----------|
| BR-CAF-001 | Ordering closes a configurable number of hours before the meal's serving time (default 2 hours); later orders are rejected with a clear message. | Validation |
| BR-CAF-003 | A meal wallet balance may not go negative when the school is in prepaid-only mode. | Validation |
| BR-CAF-008 | An order may be cancelled only before the cut-off and while it is still Confirmed; the wallet is refunded immediately on cancellation. | Workflow |
| BR-CAF-012 | A wallet deduction during ordering is performed inside a single protected transaction so two simultaneous orders cannot overspend the same balance. | Concurrency |
| BR-CAF-023 | The dish price charged on an order is fixed at the moment of ordering; later price changes do not affect existing orders. | Calculation |

#### Acceptance Criteria
1. An order can be placed only when the menu for that date is Published.
2. Orders placed after the cut-off are rejected with a descriptive message.
3. A dietary-conflict warning is shown when an ordered dish conflicts with the student's profile; an admin can override.
4. The wallet balance is deducted exactly once per order and never goes negative in prepaid-only mode.
5. An order confirmation is sent to the parent with the order summary and remaining wallet balance.
6. A student or parent can cancel before the cut-off and the wallet is refunded immediately.
7. Each order receives a unique order number in the format CAF-YYYY-XXXXXXXX.

#### Integration with Other Modules
- Receives from: StudentProfile — student identity; published menu
- Sends to: Meal wallet — deduction/refund; Notification — confirmation; Kitchen view — head-count

#### Enhancement Notes (Future)
Daily calorie/nutrition summary on the portal (see ENH-CAF-001) and a parent spend cap (see ENH-CAF-006).

### 3.9 Counter (Point-of-Sale) Interface
**Requirement ID:** REQ-CAF-009
**Priority:** Standard (P1)
**Category Tags:** [DATA_ENTRY] [WORKFLOW] [INTEGRATION]

A simplified, touch-friendly counter interface lets serving staff process walk-in purchases (tuck-shop style) independent of advance orders. - **Initiates:** Kitchen Staff, Cafeteria Manager (open session)

| BR-CAF-031 | A counter wallet transaction appears in the meal wallet ledger as a debit entry. | Calculation |

#### Acceptance Criteria
1. Staff can open a session that tracks total cash and wallet collections for the shift.
2. A student is identified by QR scan or manual lookup, and dietary flags display before items are added.
3. Staff select items from a grid of available dishes and adjust quantities.
4. Payment by wallet is deducted atomically; cash payment is recorded.
5. A receipt can be printed or sent to the parent on request.
6. Closing the session produces a daily summary reconciling wallet deductions and cash collected.
7. Counter wallet payments appear as debit entries in the wallet ledger.

#### Integration with Other Modules

- Sends to: Meal wallet — deduction; Notification — receipt (optional)

### 3.10 QR-Based Meal Attendance
**Requirement ID:** REQ-CAF-010
**Priority:** Standard (P1)
**Category Tags:** [DATA_ENTRY] [NOTIFICATION]

#### Business Description
Scanning a student's meal-card or ID QR code at the serving counter records that the student actually received a meal on that day and meal category. This is separate from ordering — it captures real consumption, which feeds the wastage report. Duplicate scans for the same student, day, and meal are ignored. Optionally, a parent receives a real-time notification when their child is scanned.

#### Actors
- **Initiates:** Kitchen Staff (scan)
- **Processes / Approves:** System (idempotency check)
- **Views / Receives notification:** Parent (optional), Cafeteria Manager, Hostel Warden

#### Business Rules
| Rule ID | Business Rule | Rule Type |
|---------|--------------|-----------|
| BR-CAF-021 | A meal-attendance record is immutable once captured; it cannot be edited or deleted. A repeated scan for the same student, day, and meal returns the existing record without error. | Validation |
| BR-CAF-032 | A parent receives a real-time scan notification only when the school has enabled the parent-scan-notification setting. | Workflow |

#### Acceptance Criteria
1. A scan records the student, meal date, meal category, scan time, and serving counter.
2. A duplicate scan for the same student, date, and meal is ignored and reported as "already recorded".
3. Attendance data feeds the wastage report (orders vs actual attendance).
4. When enabled, the parent receives a notification when their child is scanned.
5. An attendance summary shows the per-class/section attendance rate by date.

#### Integration with Other Modules
- Receives from: StudentProfile — student identity
- Sends to: Notification — scan alert (optional)

#### Enhancement Notes (Future)
None.

### 3.11 Meal Card (Prepaid Wallet) Management
**Requirement ID:** REQ-CAF-011
**Priority:** Standard (P1)
**Category Tags:** [DATA_ENTRY] [WORKFLOW] [NOTIFICATION] [INTEGRATION]

#### Business Description
Each student is issued one prepaid canteen wallet (meal card) with a unique card number and a QR code. Parents top it up online (via Razorpay) or in cash at the office. Every order or counter sale deducts from it atomically, and a complete ledger records every credit, debit, refund, and manual adjustment with the running balance after each entry. When the balance falls below a school-set threshold, the parent is notified.

#### Actors
- **Initiates:** School Admin, Accounts Staff (issue/top-up); Parent (online top-up)
- **Processes / Approves:** System (atomic balance change, webhook idempotency)
- **Views / Receives notification:** Parent (low balance, top-up confirmation), Accounts Staff

#### Business Rules
| Rule ID | Business Rule | Rule Type |
|---------|--------------|-----------|
| BR-CAF-004 | Each student may have only one active meal card; issuing a new card requires deactivating the previous one. | Validation |
| BR-CAF-011 | An online top-up confirmation must be processed only once; a repeated payment confirmation for the same payment reference is rejected. | Concurrency |

## Section 6 — Workflows

### 6.1 Weekly Menu Planning & Publish
**Trigger:** Cafeteria Manager opens the weekly planner. **End State:** Menu Published and announced (or remains Draft).

#### Steps
1. Plan: Cafeteria Manager assigns dishes to day-and-meal slots; the plan is saved as Draft.
2. Validate: System checks at least one dish is assigned.
   - Decision: If no dish assigned → publishing is blocked.
3. Publish: Admin/Manager publishes; status becomes Published and publish time/author are recorded.
4. Notify: Notification module sends the publish announcement to active students and parents.
5. Archive: When the week passes, the System archives the menu (read-only).

#### Exception Paths
- If no dishes are assigned: publish is refused with a message naming the empty slots.
- If notification dispatch fails: the menu stays Published and the alert is retried (queued).

#### Notifications Triggered
| At Step | Who Receives | Message Summary |
|---------|-------------|-----------------|
| Step 4 | Students, Parents | "Next week's cafeteria menu is now available." |

### 6.2 Meal Pre-Ordering & Confirmation
**Trigger:** Student/Parent places an order on the portal. **End State:** Order Confirmed (or rejected).

#### Steps
1. Select: Student/Parent picks a date, meal, and dishes from the published menu.
2. Validate: System checks the menu is Published and the cut-off has not passed.
   - Decision: If after cut-off → reject with a message.
3. Check diet: System checks the dietary profile.
   - Decision: If conflict → warn (admin may override; student may not).
4. Charge: System deducts the wallet inside a single protected transaction.
   - Decision: If balance insufficient in prepaid-only mode → reject.
5. Confirm: Order status becomes Confirmed with a unique order number; confirmation is sent.

#### Exception Paths
- After cut-off: order rejected.
- Insufficient balance (prepaid-only): order rejected, parent prompted to top up.
- Simultaneous orders on the same wallet: the protected transaction prevents overspend.

#### Notifications Triggered
| At Step | Who Receives | Message Summary |
|---------|-------------|-----------------|
| Step 5 | Parent | "Order CAF-… confirmed. Wallet balance: ₹…" |

### 6.3 Order Cancellation & Refund
**Trigger:** Student/Parent cancels a Confirmed order. **End State:** Order Cancelled and wallet refunded.

#### Steps
1. Request: Student/Parent requests cancellation.
2. Validate: System checks the order is Confirmed and the cut-off has not passed.
   - Decision: If after cut-off or already Served → refuse.
3. Refund: System refunds the wallet and posts a refund ledger entry.
4. Update: Order status becomes Cancelled.

#### Exception Paths
- After cut-off / already Served: cancellation refused.

#### Notifications Triggered
| At Step | Who Receives | Message Summary |
|---------|-------------|-----------------|
| Step 3 | Parent | "Order CAF-… cancelled. ₹… refunded to wallet." |

### 6.4 Meal Wallet Top-Up
**Trigger:** Parent tops up online, or Accounts Staff records a cash top-up. **End State:** Balance credited.

#### Steps
1. Initiate: Parent starts an online top-up (or staff enters a cash amount).
2. Pay: Parent completes payment at the gateway.
3. Confirm: On payment confirmation, the System verifies the confirmation and checks it has not been processed before.
   - Decision: If the payment reference was already processed → reject the duplicate.
4. Credit: System posts a credit ledger entry and updates the balance.
5. Notify: Confirmation sent to the parent.

#### Exception Paths
- Duplicate payment confirmation: rejected so the wallet is credited only once.
- Cash top-up: recorded directly by staff with an audit entry.

#### Notifications Triggered
| At Step | Who Receives | Message Summary |
|---------|-------------|-----------------|
| Step 5 | Parent | "₹… added to your child's meal wallet. Balance: ₹…" |

### 6.5 Counter (POS) Sale
**Trigger:** Staff process a walk-in purchase. **End State:** Sale recorded; session reconciled at close.

#### Steps
1. Open: Staff open the counter session for the day.
2. Identify: Staff scan the student QR or look up manually; dietary flags display.
3. Sell: Staff add items; staff take payment by wallet (atomic deduction) or cash.
4. Record: A transaction (and, for wallet, a ledger debit) is recorded.
5. Close: At shift end, the session is closed and totals are reconciled.

#### Exception Paths
- Transaction attempted with no open session: refused.
- A second session opened the same day: refused (one open session per day).

#### Notifications Triggered
| At Step | Who Receives | Message Summary |
|---------|-------------|-----------------|
| Step 4 | Parent (on request) | "Cafeteria purchase of ₹… on your child's wallet." |

### 6.6 QR Meal Attendance Scan
**Trigger:** Staff scan a student at the serving counter. **End State:** Attendance recorded once.

#### Steps
1. Scan: Staff scan the student's QR at the counter.
2. Check: System checks whether this student/date/meal is already recorded.
   - Decision: If already recorded → return "already recorded" without error.
3. Record: System stores an immutable attendance record.
4. Notify (optional): If enabled, the parent is notified.

#### Exception Paths
- Duplicate scan: silently treated as already recorded.

#### Notifications Triggered
| At Step | Who Receives | Message Summary |
|---------|-------------|-----------------|
| Step 4 | Parent (if enabled) | "Your child was served Lunch at the cafeteria." |

### 6.7 Stock Reorder Alert
**Trigger:** Consumption log reduces stock to or below the reorder level. **End State:** Alert raised (and optional purchase request).

#### Steps
1. Log: Kitchen Staff log consumption; stock reduces.
2. Check: System compares quantity to the reorder level.
   - Decision: If at/below reorder level → raise alert.
3. Alert: In-app alert to all Cafeteria Managers.
4. Hand-off (optional): If Inventory integration is enabled, raise a purchase request.

#### Exception Paths
- Inventory not licensed: alert only, no purchase request.

#### Notifications Triggered
| At Step | Who Receives | Message Summary |
|---------|-------------|-----------------|
| Step 3 | Cafeteria Managers | "Low stock: [item] is at/below its reorder level." |

### 6.8 Hostel Mess Auto-Enrolment
**Trigger:** A student is admitted to the hostel (Hostel module event). **End State:** Mess-plan enrolment created and charged.

#### Steps
1. Admit: Hostel module signals a new hostel admission.
2. Enrol: System creates a hostel-mess-plan enrolment for the student.
3. Charge: The plan price is deducted from the student's wallet.

#### Exception Paths
- Auto-enrolment setting disabled: no enrolment created.
- Insufficient wallet balance: enrolment created and flagged for top-up per school policy.

#### Notifications Triggered
| At Step | Who Receives | Message Summary |
|---------|-------------|-----------------|
| Step 2 | Parent, Hostel Warden | "Your child has been enrolled in the hostel mess plan." |

### 6.9 FSSAI Licence Expiry Reminder
**Trigger:** Scheduled check against licence expiry dates. **End State:** Reminder sent.

#### Steps
1. Check: System checks school and supplier FSSAI expiry dates daily.
2. Decide: School licence at 60 and 30 days before expiry; supplier licence at 30 and 7 days before expiry.
3. Remind: Reminder sent to the School Admin.

#### Exception Paths
- No expiry date recorded: no reminder.

#### Notifications Triggered
| At Step | Who Receives | Message Summary |
|---------|-------------|-----------------|
| Step 3 | School Admin | "FSSAI licence for [entity] expires in [N] days." |

## Section 9 — Non-Functional Requirements

### 9.1 Performance Expectations
| Requirement | Standard |
|-------------|---------|
| Screen load time | All screens load within 3 seconds for up to 500 concurrent users |
| Report generation | Standard reports complete within 10 seconds |
| Large reports | Reports with 1,000+ rows complete within 30 seconds |
| Kitchen view | Consolidation of 500+ orders for a date/meal returns within 2 seconds |
| Concurrent ordering | Simultaneous wallet deductions never overspend a balance |

### 9.2 Security Requirements (Business Language)
| Requirement | Rule |
|-------------|------|
| Access control | Only users with the correct role may access each screen and action |
| Data isolation | One school's cafeteria data is never visible to another school |
| Self-service scoping | Students see only their own orders/balance; parents see only their child's data |
| Confidential diet data | Medical-diet notes are visible only to kitchen staff and authorised admins |
| Financial integrity | Every wallet credit, debit, refund, and adjustment records who made it and when, and the ledger cannot be edited or deleted |
| Payment idempotency | An online top-up confirmation can credit a wallet only once |
| Audit trail | All state changes (publish, order, cancel, top-up, enrol) are logged |

### 9.3 Usability Requirements
| Requirement | Standard |
|-------------|---------|
| Mobile access | Ordering, wallet, and menu screens work on mobile browsers |
| Touch counter | The counter interface is touch-friendly for tablet use |
| Currency & units | Prices shown in INR (₹); nutrition in grams/kcal |
| Language | All labels and messages in English (Hindi/regional as a future enhancement) |
| Clear cut-off feedback | The ordering screen shows the cut-off time and rejects late orders with a clear reason |
