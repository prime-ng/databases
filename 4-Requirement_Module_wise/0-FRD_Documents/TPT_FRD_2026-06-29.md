## Section 1 — Module Overview

### 1.1 Business Purpose

Most Indian K-12 schools run a fleet of buses and vans that carry hundreds of children between home and school twice a day. Today this is managed on paper and spreadsheets: who is allocated to which bus and stop, which driver has a valid licence, whether a vehicle's fitness and insurance are current, whether a child actually boarded the bus this morning, and how much transport fee is outstanding. The Transport module digitises the entire school-transport operation — vehicles and their compliance documents, routes and stops, drivers and helpers, daily trips, QR/RFID-based student boarding attendance, vehicle inspection and maintenance, and transport fee collection — giving the school a single, auditable, real-time view of its fleet. Above all, it answers the question every parent and principal cares about most: *is the child safely on the right bus, and did they reach their stop?*

### 1.2 Business Value

- **Child safety & accountability:** Every student's boarding and un-boarding is captured by a device scan at the stop, with a parent notification — a verifiable safety trail that paper registers cannot provide.
- **Regulatory compliance:** Vehicle fitness, insurance, pollution, and fire-extinguisher certificates are tracked with expiry alerts; a non-compliant or unsafe vehicle is automatically taken out of service — directly supporting CBSE/State transport-safety norms.
- **Driver fitness:** Licence expiry and police-verification status are monitored so an unverified or unlicensed driver is never silently assigned to a trip.
- **Fee recovery:** Route-wise monthly fees, fines, and collections are tracked per student, surfacing outstanding and overdue amounts the school would otherwise lose.
- **Operational efficiency:** Routes, stops, schedules, and daily trips are planned once and executed repeatably; maintenance flows through a controlled inspection→service→repair pipeline instead of ad-hoc spend.
- **Data privacy by design:** Driver Aadhaar/ID numbers and student location data are treated as sensitive personal data under the DPDP Act and handled accordingly.

### 1.3 Scope

#### In Scope
1. Vehicle registry with compliance documents, expiry tracking, and availability status
2. Route, stop, and route-stop network setup (with map geometry)
3. Driver and helper (personnel) profiles, licence and police-verification tracking
4. Shift configuration and driver-route-vehicle assignment
5. Route scheduling templates and daily trip lifecycle (with stop-by-stop tracking, incidents, and approval)
6. Student boarding/un-boarding attendance via QR/RFID device scan, with parent notification
7. Driver attendance via device scan or manual entry
8. Vehicle pre-trip inspection, service request, and maintenance lifecycle
9. Vehicle fuel logging with approval and efficiency calculation
10. Student transport allocation per academic session, with stop-change workflow
11. Transport fee schedule, fines, collection, and outstanding tracking
12. Compliance/licence expiry alerts (scheduled)
13. Eleven operational and financial reports plus a management dashboard

#### Out of Scope
1. **Live GPS tracking** — the data structures exist but third-party GPS-hardware integration is a future enhancement (REQ-TPT-022)
2. **ML route optimisation** — data models only; no inference/training (REQ-TPT-023)
3. **Accounting vouchers** — posting fee receipts and maintenance expense to the ledger is owned by the Accounting module (deferred integration)
4. **Vendor billing internals** — vendor bills and usage logs are owned by the Vendor module; Transport only raises the trigger
5. **Parent-facing bus-location app** — the Parent Portal module owns the parent UI; Transport owns the data
6. **Actual message delivery** — push/SMS/WhatsApp delivery is performed by the Notification module; Transport composes and logs the events

### 1.4 Key Terminology

| Business Term | Meaning |
|---------------|---------|
| Shift | A transport operating window (e.g., Morning, Afternoon, Evening) that groups routes, stops, and assignments |
| Route | An ordered chain of stops a vehicle follows, in a Pickup or Drop direction |
| Stop (Pickup Point) | A boarding/alighting location with a map position and a fare |
| Vehicle | A school bus, van, or car with capacity, ownership, and compliance documents |
| Compliance Document | A statutory certificate for a vehicle — fitness, insurance, pollution, fire-extinguisher — each with an expiry date |
| Personnel | A driver, helper, or transport manager record (licence, ID, police verification) |
| Assignment | A dated link of driver + helper + vehicle to a route and shift |
| Route Scheduler | A recurring schedule template from which daily trips are generated |
| Trip | A single execution of a route on a date, with start/end odometer, fuel, and a status lifecycle |
| Student Allocation | A student's assigned pickup and drop route + stop for an academic session |
| Boarding Log | A record that a specific student boarded/un-boarded a specific trip at a specific stop, captured by a device scan |
| Attendance Device | A registered QR scanner / RFID reader used to capture boarding and driver-attendance scans |
| Inspection | A pre-trip safety checklist (15 checkpoints) producing Passed/Failed |
| Service Request | A request to repair a vehicle, raised from a failed inspection or reported issue, requiring approval |
| Maintenance | The actual repair record, created only from an approved service request |
| Fine Rule (Fine Master) | A late-payment penalty definition (day-range, fixed or percentage rate) per academic session |
| Transport Fee | The monthly charge for a student's bus usage, with optional fines for late payment |
| Availability Status | Whether a vehicle is currently fit and free to be assigned to a trip |

## Section 3 — Functional Requirements

### 3.1 Shift Configuration
**Requirement ID:** REQ-TPT-001 | **Priority:** Standard (P1) | **Category Tags:** [CONFIGURATION]

#### Business Description
The Transport Manager defines the operating shifts (e.g., Morning, Afternoon, Evening) with a code, name, and effective date range. Shifts group routes, stops, and assignments so the same physical fleet can run different patterns at different times of day. Shifts can be soft-deleted and restored.

#### Actors
- **Initiates / Processes:** Transport Manager, School Admin
- **Views:** All transport configuration screens (shift appears as a selector)

#### Business Rules
| Rule ID | Business Rule | Rule Type |
|---------|--------------|-----------|
| BR-TPT-015 | Shift, route, and stop codes and names must each be unique within the school. | Validation |

#### Acceptance Criteria
1. A shift can be created with a code, name, and effective-from/effective-to dates
2. A shift appears as a selectable option on routes, stops, and assignments
3. A shift can be soft-deleted, listed in trash, and restored
4. A standard set of shifts is available for a new school (seeded)

#### Integration with Other Modules
- None (internal configuration)

#### Enhancement Notes (Future)
- Standard shift seeder for new schools (ENH-TPT-005)

### 3.2 Vehicle Registry & Compliance Documents
**Requirement ID:** REQ-TPT-002 | **Priority:** Core (P0) | **Category Tags:** [DATA_ENTRY] [CONFIGURATION]

#### Business Description
The Transport Manager registers every vehicle in the fleet with its registration details, type (bus/van/car), fuel type, emission class, ownership (owned/leased/rented, with a vendor link for hired vehicles), seating and maximum capacity, and GPS device identifier. For each vehicle the school uploads and tracks statutory compliance documents — fitness, insurance, pollution, and fire-extinguisher certificates — each with an expiry date. A vehicle carries an availability status that governs whether it can be assigned to a trip.

#### Actors
- **Initiates / Processes:** Transport Manager, School Admin
- **Views / Receives notification:** Transport Manager (expiry alerts — see REQ-TPT-019)

#### Business Rules
| Rule ID | Business Rule | Rule Type |
|---------|--------------|-----------|
| BR-TPT-002 | A vehicle with any expired compliance document (fitness, insurance, pollution, fire-extinguisher) must not be assignable to a new trip; its availability status is automatically set to unavailable when any document expires. | Workflow |
| BR-TPT-025 | When a vehicle returns from maintenance (out-of-service date recorded), its availability status is restored to available. | Workflow |

#### Acceptance Criteria
1. A vehicle can be registered with type, fuel, ownership, capacity, and (for hired vehicles) a vendor link
2. Each compliance document can be uploaded with an expiry date, and the upload status is visible
3. A vehicle whose any document has expired shows as unavailable and cannot be picked when assigning a trip
4. A vehicle can be soft-deleted, restored, and force-deleted

#### Integration with Other Modules
- Receives from: Vendor — vendor record for hired vehicles; GlobalMaster — vehicle/fuel/ownership/emission value lists
- Sends to: Trip Management (REQ-TPT-009) — availability gate

#### Enhancement Notes (Future)
- Auto-set availability via a scheduled expiry job (covered in REQ-TPT-019)

### 3.3 Route & Stop Network Setup
**Requirement ID:** REQ-TPT-003 | **Priority:** Core (P0) | **Category Tags:** [CONFIGURATION] [DATA_ENTRY]

#### Business Description
The Transport Manager builds the route network: routes (with a direction — pickup or drop — and a shift), stops (with map coordinates and a fare), and the assignment of stops to routes in a fixed sequence. Each route-stop assignment carries the arrival/departure offset, estimated time, and the one-side and both-side fares used later for student allocation. Route geometry is stored for map display.

#### Actors
- **Initiates / Processes:** Transport Manager, School Admin
- **Views:** Allocation and trip screens (route/stop selectors)

#### Business Rules
| Rule ID | Business Rule | Rule Type |
|---------|--------------|-----------|
| BR-TPT-015 | Route and stop codes and names must be unique within the school. | Validation |
| BR-TPT-016 | A given stop may appear on a given route only once; stops on a route are held in an explicit display sequence. | Validation |

#### Acceptance Criteria
1. A route can be created with direction, shift, and map geometry
2. A stop can be created with coordinates, stop type, and fare
3. Stops can be assigned to a route in an ordered sequence with per-stop fares; the same stop cannot be added to the route twice
4. Routes and stops support soft-delete and restore

#### Integration with Other Modules
- Receives from: GlobalMaster — direction/stop-type value lists
- Sends to: Student Allocation (REQ-TPT-016), Trip Management (REQ-TPT-009)

#### Enhancement Notes (Future)
- Map-based drag-to-sequence stop ordering

### 3.4 Driver & Helper Management

#### Business Description
The Transport Manager maintains profiles for drivers, helpers, and transport managers: personal details, a scan code (QR/RFID) for attendance, government ID type and number, driving licence number and expiry, driving experience, police-verification status, address, and supporting documents. Personnel can be linked to a system login for app-based attendance. Government ID numbers (e.g., Aadhaar) are sensitive personal data and are protected accordingly.

#### Actors
- **Initiates / Processes:** Transport Manager, School Admin

#### Business Rules
| Rule ID | Business Rule | Rule Type |
|---------|--------------|-----------|
| BR-TPT-003 | A driver whose driving licence has expired must not be assignable as the primary driver of a new trip. | Workflow |
| BR-TPT-004 | A driver/helper without completed police verification is flagged; whether this only warns or hard-blocks trip assignment is a configurable school policy. | Permission |

1. A driver/helper can be registered with licence details, ID, police-verification status, and a scan code

### 3.9 Daily Trip Management
**Requirement ID:** REQ-TPT-009 | **Priority:** Core (P0) | **Category Tags:** [WORKFLOW] [APPROVAL]

#### Business Description
A trip is a single run of a route on a date. It records the vehicle, driver, helper, direction, start/end times, start/end odometer, and start/end fuel. As the bus runs, each stop is marked reached with arrival/departure times. The trip moves through a one-directional lifecycle — Scheduled → In Progress → Completed → Approved — and only an authorised user can approve it. Trip approval can trigger a vendor usage-log entry for hired vehicles.

#### Actors
- **Initiates:** Transport Manager (creates), Driver (executes/records stop events)
- **Processes / Approves:** Transport Manager (approval)
- **Views:** Dashboard, reports

#### Business Rules
| Rule ID | Business Rule | Rule Type |
|---------|--------------|-----------|
| BR-TPT-005 | Trip status transitions are one-directional: Scheduled → In Progress → Completed → Approved. A completed trip cannot revert to in-progress. | Workflow |
| BR-TPT-006 | Only a user with trip-approval permission may set a trip to Approved. | Permission |
| BR-TPT-013 | When a trip is approved and the school setting for vendor usage logging is enabled, a usage-log entry is raised for the hired vehicle. | Workflow |
| BR-TPT-002 | A trip may only be created with a vehicle whose availability status is available (compliant). | Workflow |

#### Acceptance Criteria
1. A trip can be created from a schedule with vehicle, driver, and direction in status Scheduled
2. The trip records start/end odometer and fuel and per-stop reached times
3. Status only advances in the allowed order; an illegal transition is blocked
4. Only an authorised user can approve a trip; approval is timestamped and attributed
5. A non-available (non-compliant) vehicle cannot be used for a trip

#### Integration with Other Modules
- Receives from: Scheduler (REQ-TPT-008), Vehicle (REQ-TPT-002), Personnel (REQ-TPT-004)
- Sends to: Vendor — usage log on approval (conditional); Boarding (REQ-TPT-011)

#### Enhancement Notes (Future)
- Auto trip generation (ENH-TPT-004); Accounting expense integration (ENH-TPT-006)

### 3.11 Student Boarding Attendance
**Requirement ID:** REQ-TPT-011 | **Priority:** Core (P0) | **Category Tags:** [WORKFLOW] [NOTIFICATION] [INTEGRATION]

#### Business Description
At each stop, the driver or helper scans the student's QR/RFID card on a registered device. The system validates that the student has an active allocation for this route, stop, and session, and that the device is registered and active; on a valid scan it records the boarding (or un-boarding on the return trip) and triggers a notification to the parent. This is the module's child-safety backbone — a verifiable record that the right child boarded the right bus at the right stop.

#### Actors
- **Initiates:** Driver, Helper (scan)
- **Processes / Approves:** System (validation, logging, notification)
- **Views / Receives notification:** Parent (boarding/trip alert), Transport Manager

#### Business Rules
| Rule ID | Business Rule | Rule Type |
|---------|--------------|-----------|
| BR-TPT-014 | A boarding scan is accepted only if the student has an active allocation for the current session, the scanned stop matches the student's allocated stop for the route direction, and the device is registered and active. A repeat scan on the same trip is marked a duplicate, not a new boarding. | Validation |

#### Acceptance Criteria
1. A valid scan records a boarding/un-boarding with the trip, stop, time, and device
2. A scan for an unallocated student, a wrong stop, or an unregistered device is rejected
3. A duplicate scan on the same trip is flagged as duplicate and does not create a second boarding
4. A parent notification event is created on a valid boarding

#### Integration with Other Modules
- Receives from: Student Allocation (REQ-TPT-016), Attendance Device (REQ-TPT-005), Trip (REQ-TPT-009)
- Sends to: Notification module — parent boarding alerts; Parent/Student Profile — student lookup

#### Enhancement Notes (Future)
- Student event-log audit trail (ENH-TPT-010); push/SMS/WhatsApp channels (ENH-TPT-003)

### 3.14 Vehicle Service Request & Maintenance
**Requirement ID:** REQ-TPT-014 | **Priority:** Standard (P1) | **Category Tags:** [WORKFLOW] [APPROVAL]

#### Business Description
A service request (from a failed inspection or a reported issue) is reviewed and approved by the Transport Manager. Only an approved service request can produce a maintenance record — the actual repair, with cost, workshop, in/out-service dates, and next-due date. While a vehicle has an open service request it stays out of service; when maintenance completes (out-of-service date recorded) the vehicle returns to available, and the maintenance cost is raised as a vendor bill.

#### Actors
- **Initiates:** Driver/Transport Manager (request)
- **Processes / Approves:** Transport Manager (approval)
- **Views:** Cost & maintenance report

#### Business Rules
| Rule ID | Business Rule | Rule Type |
|---------|--------------|-----------|
| BR-TPT-008 | A maintenance record can only be created from an approved service request; direct creation of maintenance is not allowed. | Workflow |
| BR-TPT-012 | When a maintenance record is approved, a corresponding vendor bill for the maintenance cost is raised. | Workflow |
| BR-TPT-025 | Recording the out-of-service date restores the vehicle's availability to available. | Workflow |

#### Acceptance Criteria
1. A service request can be approved or rejected; approval is attributed and timestamped

## Section 6 — Workflows

### 6.1 Student Boarding (QR/RFID Scan)
**Trigger:** Driver/helper scans a student at a stop. **End:** Boarding logged + parent notified.
**Steps:** 1. Scan on a registered device. 2. System validates device active + student has active allocation for this route/stop/session. 3. Valid → record boarding (or un-boarding on return); invalid → reject; repeat → mark duplicate. 4. Raise parent notification event. 5. (Future) write student event-log audit.
**Exceptions:** unregistered device / unallocated student / wrong stop → rejected; duplicate scan → no second boarding.
**Notifications:** Parent — "Your child boarded/alighted at [stop] at [time]."

### 6.2 Trip Lifecycle
**Trigger:** Schedule template exists for the date. **End:** Trip Approved.
**Steps:** 1. Manager creates trip (validates vehicle available + driver licence valid) → Scheduled. 2. Driver departs → In Progress (start time/odometer/fuel). 3. Each stop → reached time recorded; scans drive boarding (6.1). 4. Trip ends at school → Completed (end time/odometer/fuel). 5. Manager approves → Approved; if vendor-usage setting on → raise vendor usage log.
**Exceptions:** non-available vehicle or expired-licence driver blocks creation; illegal status jump blocked.
**Notifications:** Parent — trip start/approaching/reached/delayed/cancelled.

### 6.3 Vehicle Inspection → Service → Maintenance
**Trigger:** Pre-trip inspection. **End:** Vehicle back in service.
**Steps:** 1. Inspection recorded; Passed → trip proceeds. 2. Failed → auto service request + vehicle unavailable. 3. Manager approves request → maintenance record opened. 4. Repair done; out-of-service date set → vehicle available + vendor bill raised.
**Exceptions:** no maintenance without an approved request; vehicle stays unavailable while a request is open.

### 6.4 Driver Attendance
**Trigger:** Driver scans/manual entry. **End:** Day's attendance computed.
**Steps:** 1. First scan → in-time (one record/day). 2. Last scan → out-time; work minutes computed; status derived. 3. Absent → manager marks; trip flagged for reassignment.

### 6.5 Student Allocation & Stop Change
**Trigger:** Allocation or stop-change request. **End:** Active allocation reflects the latest stop.
**Steps:** 1. Allocate — check capacity, deactivate prior active record, insert new (atomic). 2. Stop-change request raised by parent/admin. 3. Manager approves → new allocation effective from approval date; prior deactivated.
**Exceptions:** capacity full → blocked; 90% → warning.

### 6.6 Transport Fee Charging & Collection
**Trigger:** Monthly fee run / payment. **End:** Payment recorded, outstanding updated.
**Steps:** 1. Monthly fee generated per allocated student from the fare. 2. Late payment → fine computed from rules. 3. Collection recorded (date/mode/amount); outstanding updated; payment written to audit log. 4. (Future) receipt voucher posted to Accounting.
**Exceptions:** mid-month route change → proration per school policy.
