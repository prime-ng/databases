## Section 1 — Module Overview

### 1.1 Business Purpose

Residential (boarding) schools in India house anywhere from 50 to over 2,000 students on campus, and the school is legally and morally responsible for the safety, accommodation, food, health, discipline, and well-being of every boarder around the clock. The Hostel module gives a school a single place to allocate students to beds, take daily roll calls, control who leaves and returns to campus, run the mess kitchen, recover hostel charges, and keep the discipline, visitor, sick-bay and maintenance records that boarding inspections demand. Without it, wardens manage hundreds of children using paper registers, parents are not alerted when a child is absent or unwell, and the school cannot demonstrate compliance during a CBSE/ICSE boarding inspection.

### 1.2 Business Value

- **Student safety and parent confidence** — every absence, late return, incident or sick-bay admission automatically alerts the parent within minutes, not the next day.
- **Eliminates paper registers** — allotment, attendance, leave, movement, visitor, mess and discipline registers become searchable, exportable digital records.
- **Inspection-ready compliance** — boarding inspectors can be shown attendance registers, leave registers, incident logs, visitor logs and occupancy reports on demand.
- **Accurate, automated billing** — room rent, mess, electricity, laundry and deposits are calculated (including mid-month proration) and pushed to the fee system without manual re-keying.
- **Scoped accountability** — each warden sees only the students and floors assigned to them, and the system always records which warden was on duty when an event occurred.
- **Operational visibility at scale** — pre-computed occupancy and attendance counts let a 500-plus student hostel load its dashboard instantly.

### 1.3 Scope

#### In Scope

1. Hostel infrastructure setup — buildings, floors, rooms and beds in a Building → Floor → Room → Bed hierarchy, with gender restriction.
2. Configuration masters — room types, bed types, and the configurable status codes used across the module.
3. Warden management — chief, block and floor warden assignment with rotation history, scoped data access, and a daily on-duty duty roster.
4. Student room allotment, vacating, transfer, and academic-year bulk reset; pre-admission room reservation; room-change request workflow.
5. Daily roll-call attendance (morning / evening / night) with bulk-marking, late-entry capture, locking, and absence alerts.
6. In-out movement (gate-pass) register with pending-return tracking and overdue alerts.
7. Leave-pass application, approval, return confirmation, late-return detection, and printable gate pass.
8. Mess management — weekly menu, special-diet assignment, meal attendance, per-meal opt-outs, and monthly mess billing.
9. Hostel fee structure configuration, automatic fee calculation with proration, and pushing hostel charges to the fee system.
10. Discipline incident register with severity, escalation, warning-letter generation, and repeat-offender flagging.
11. Hostel-internal complaint/maintenance register with assignment and SLA-based escalation.
12. Visitor register, sick-bay (medical room) management, room inventory, bed maintenance, housekeeping and laundry tracking.
13. Hostel-level emergency contacts (doctor, ambulance, fire).
14. Dashboard and a reporting suite covering occupancy, attendance, leave, movement, fee, discipline, mess, inventory, maintenance, laundry, visitor and medical reporting.
15. A module-internal audit trail and notification-delivery log.

#### Out of Scope

1. Student academic profile, class, section and marks — handled by the **StudentProfile** module.
2. School fee collection, receipts and online payment — handled by the **StudentFee** and **Payment** modules (Hostel only computes and hands over the charge).
3. Full medical history and hospital case management — handled by the **HPC** module (the sick-bay log keeps only a referral link).
4. Day-scholar canteen / school-wide mess — handled separately; the Hostel mess is exclusive to boarders.
5. Campus-wide visitor and gate management for non-boarders — handled by the **FrontOffice** module.
6. School-wide grievances unrelated to hostel operations — handled by the **Complaint** module (the hostel complaint register is hostel-internal only).
7. Staff quarters / employee accommodation, CCTV and access-control hardware, and outside vendor/contractor management.

### 1.4 Key Terminology

| Business Term | Meaning |
|---------------|---------|
| Hostel / Building | A residential building operated by the school, designated as boys', girls' or mixed. |
| Floor | A level within a hostel that groups rooms and is usually assigned a floor incharge. |
| Room | A lettered/numbered room on a floor, of a defined room type and bed capacity. |
| Bed | An individual sleeping place inside a room; the unit a student is actually allotted to. |

| Room Reservation | A pre-admission hold on a room/bed for a prospective student before their record exists. |

| Leave Pass | A formal, approved authorisation for a boarder to be away from the hostel for one or more days. |

| Fee Structure | The configured monthly rates (room rent, mess, electricity, laundry, deposit) for a room type and meal plan. |
| Fee Demand | A hostel charge raised against a student and handed to the fee system for collection. |
| Incident | A recorded disciplinary or behavioural event involving a boarder, with a severity. |
| Warning Letter | A formal printable letter issued to a boarder/parent following an incident. |
| Sick Bay | The on-premises medical room where a boarder is admitted for minor illness. |

## Section 3 — Functional Requirements

### 3.1 Hostel / Building Configuration
**Requirement ID:** REQ-HST-001
**Priority:** Core (P0)
**Category Tags:** [DATA_ENTRY] [CONFIGURATION]

#### Business Description
The School Admin sets up each hostel the school operates — its name, short code, gender type (boys', girls' or mixed), address, contact number, chief warden, sick-bay bed count, and visiting-day rules. A school may run several hostels simultaneously (separate boys' and girls' buildings, old and new blocks). Total capacity is kept up to date automatically as beds are added or removed. A hostel can be deactivated only when no students are currently living in it.

#### Actors
- **Initiates:** School Admin
- **Processes / Approves:** School Admin
- **Views / Receives notification:** Chief Warden, all hostel staff

#### Business Rules
| Rule ID | Business Rule | Rule Type |
|---------|--------------|-----------|
| BR-HST-009 | A hostel cannot be deactivated while it still has any active allotments. | Validation |
| BR-HST-033 | A hostel may have only one current chief warden; assigning a new chief warden automatically ends the previous assignment. | Workflow |

#### Acceptance Criteria
1. A School Admin can create a hostel with name, gender type, code, address, contact and sick-bay capacity, and it appears in the hostel list.
2. The system rejects deactivating a hostel that has any students currently allotted, with a clear message.
3. Total capacity displayed for a hostel matches the number of usable beds beneath it.
4. A school can maintain more than one active hostel at the same time.

#### Integration with Other Modules
- Receives from: System (staff records for the chief warden).
- Sends to: None.

#### Enhancement Notes (Future)
Facilities and visiting-day rules are currently free-form lists; a future version may normalise these into selectable master options (ENH-HST-006).

### 3.8 Student Bed Allotment
**Requirement ID:** REQ-HST-008
**Priority:** Core (P0)
**Category Tags:** [DATA_ENTRY] [WORKFLOW] [INTEGRATION]

#### Business Description
A warden allots a boarder to a specific bed by selecting the student, navigating hostel → floor → room → bed, choosing a meal plan, and setting an allotment date within an academic session. The system enforces that the bed and the student are each free, that the student's gender matches the hostel, and that a fee structure exists for the chosen room type and meal plan; it then computes the applicable hostel fee and hands it to the fee system. Students can be vacated (with a prorated refund if mid-month), transferred to another bed, or bulk-vacated at the end of an academic year.

#### Actors
- **Initiates:** Warden / School Admin
- **Processes / Approves:** Warden / School Admin
- **Views / Receives notification:** Accountant (fee), Student/Parent (future)

#### Business Rules
| Rule ID | Business Rule | Rule Type |
|---------|--------------|-----------|
| BR-HST-001 | A bed can have only one active allotment at a time. | Concurrency |
| BR-HST-002 | A student can have only one active allotment at a time. | Concurrency |
| BR-HST-003 | The student's gender must match the hostel type before an allotment can be created. | Validation |
| BR-HST-015 | A fee structure must exist for the chosen room type and meal plan before an allotment can be created. | Validation |
| BR-HST-014 | An academic-year bulk vacate requires explicit typed confirmation and is written to the audit trail; it cannot be undone. | Workflow |

#### Acceptance Criteria
1. A warden can allot a free bed to a student whose gender matches the hostel, and the bed becomes Occupied.
2. The system refuses a second active allotment for the same bed or the same student.
3. An allotment is blocked when no fee structure exists for the selected room type and meal plan.
4. Vacating a student mid-month frees the bed and triggers a prorated refund to the fee system.
5. A bulk year-end vacate runs only after typed confirmation and records an audit entry.

#### Integration with Other Modules
- Receives from: StudentProfile (student record, gender); SchoolSetup (academic session).
- Sends to: StudentFee (hostel fee demand, prorated refund).

#### Enhancement Notes (Future)
Waitlisting a student for a full preferred room with automatic notification when a bed frees up (ENH-HST-005).

### 3.11 Daily Roll-Call Attendance
**Requirement ID:** REQ-HST-011
**Priority:** Core (P0)
**Category Tags:** [DATA_ENTRY] [WORKFLOW] [NOTIFICATION]

#### Business Description
For each hostel, on each date, for each shift (morning / evening / night), a warden runs a roll call. There is one session per hostel/date/shift. The warden can mark all boarders present and then correct exceptions, recording each student as present, absent, on leave, on home leave, late or in the sick bay, with remarks and a check-in time for late entries. A student marked absent who is not on leave or in the sick bay immediately alerts the parent and the warden dashboard. Each session stores its own present/absent/leave/late counts so reports load instantly even for large hostels, and a session can be edited only by the chief warden within 24 hours before it locks.

#### Actors
- **Initiates:** Warden (on-duty)
- **Processes / Approves:** Chief Warden (corrections/lock)
- **Views / Receives notification:** Parent (absence alert), Chief Warden

#### Business Rules
| Rule ID | Business Rule | Rule Type |
|---------|--------------|-----------|
| BR-HST-007 | An attendance session is unique per hostel, date and shift; re-creating one returns the existing session rather than a duplicate. | Concurrency |
| BR-HST-017 | A student absent at roll call who is not on leave and not in the sick bay triggers a parent notification. | Workflow |
| BR-HST-018 | Students whose hostel attendance falls below the configured threshold (default 90%) are flagged on the dashboard. | Calculation |
| BR-HST-030 | A saved attendance session can be edited only by the chief warden within 24 hours, after which it is locked. | Workflow |

#### Acceptance Criteria
1. A warden can open one roll-call session per hostel/date/shift and bulk-mark all present.
2. Attempting to open a second session for the same hostel/date/shift returns the existing one.
3. Marking a student absent (not on leave/sick bay) sends a parent alert and shows on the dashboard.
4. After 24 hours the session is read-only for everyone.

#### Integration with Other Modules
- Receives from: StudentProfile (boarder list).
- Sends to: Notification (absence alert to parent).

#### Enhancement Notes (Future)
None.

### 3.13 Leave Pass Management
**Requirement ID:** REQ-HST-013
**Priority:** Core (P0)
**Category Tags:** [WORKFLOW] [APPROVAL] [NOTIFICATION]

#### Business Description
A boarder (or staff on their behalf) applies for leave with type, from/to dates, destination, purpose and a guardian contact. A block or chief warden approves or rejects it; rejection requires a reason. On approval the parent is notified, and every roll-call shift and every meal in the leave period is automatically marked as on-leave so the student is not falsely shown absent. ### 3.19 Fee Assignment, Proration & Demand Push
**Requirement ID:** REQ-HST-019
**Priority:** Core (P0)
**Category Tags:** [CALCULATION] [INTEGRATION]

#### Business Description
When a student is allotted, the system looks up the active fee structure for the room type and meal plan and computes the total monthly charge; if the allotment starts mid-month it charges only the remaining days (prorated). | BR-HST-032 | A damage-recovery charge can be pushed to fees only after a responsible student is identified. | Validation |

#### Acceptance Criteria
1. A mid-month allotment charges only the remaining days using the (rate ÷ 30 × days) formula.
2. A room change pushes a credit for the old room and a charge for the new room.
3. The fee-defaulter view lists every student with an outstanding hostel balance.
4. Each charge raised is recorded locally and linked to its fee-system counterpart.

## Section 6 — Workflows

### 6.1 Student Room Allocation Workflow
**Trigger:** A warden/admin allots a boarder to a bed.
**End State:** Active allotment created, bed occupied, hostel fee handed to the fee system.

#### Steps
1. Warden searches for the student.
2. System checks gender matches the hostel type.
   - Decision: If gender mismatch → block with message.
3. Warden browses hostel → floor → room → available bed and selects one.
4. Warden selects a meal plan and allotment date.
5. System validates the bed and student are each free and a fee structure exists.
   - Decision: If any check fails → block and explain.
6. System creates the active allotment, marks the bed occupied, increments occupancy, and marks the room full if capacity is reached.
7. System computes the hostel fee (prorated if mid-month) and pushes it to the fee system.

#### Exception Paths
- If no fee structure exists for the room type/meal plan: allotment is blocked until one is configured.
- If the bed becomes occupied by a concurrent action: the second allotment is refused.

#### Notifications Triggered
| At Step | Who Receives | Message Summary |
|---------|-------------|-----------------|
| 7 | Accountant / Parent (future) | A hostel fee has been raised for the student. |

### 6.2 Room Change Request Workflow
**Trigger:** A student/staff submits a room-change request.
**End State:** Request approved (student transferred) or rejected.

#### Steps
1. Requester submits current allotment, optional target room, and reason.
2. Warden reviews the request.
3. Decision: Approve → system vacates the old bed, creates a new allotment in the target bed, recalculates the fee difference, and links the new allotment to the request.
4. Decision: Reject → system records the rejection reason.

#### Exception Paths
- If the target bed is no longer available at approval time: warden must pick another bed or the request is held.

#### Notifications Triggered
| At Step | Who Receives | Message Summary |
|---------|-------------|-----------------|
| 3/4 | Requesting student/staff | Your room-change request was approved/rejected. |

### 6.3 Leave Pass Approval & Return Workflow
**Trigger:** A leave application is submitted.
**End State:** Student returned (on time, or late with auto-incident), or leave rejected/cancelled.

#### Steps
1. Applicant submits leave details (type, dates, destination, purpose, guardian contact).
2. Warden reviews; optionally views outstanding fees (advisory).
3. Decision: Approve → mark all covered shifts and meals on-leave, notify the parent, make the gate pass printable.
4. Decision: Reject → record reason and notify the applicant.
5. On return, warden marks the student returned with the actual date.
   - Decision: If actual return date is after the leave end date → auto-create a late-arrival incident.

#### Exception Paths
- If end date precedes start date: application is rejected at submission.
- If the student does not return: the pass remains open and shows on pending follow-ups.

#### Notifications Triggered
| At Step | Who Receives | Message Summary |
|---------|-------------|-----------------|
| 3 | Parent | Leave approved: dates and destination. |
| 4 | Applicant | Leave rejected, with reason. |
| 5 | Parent | Student returned safely (and incident note if late). |

### 6.4 Daily Roll-Call Attendance & Absence Alert Workflow
**Trigger:** A warden starts a roll call for a hostel/date/shift.
**End State:** Session saved with counts; parents of unexplained absentees alerted.

#### Steps
1. System opens (or returns the existing) session for the hostel/date/shift.
2. Warden bulk-marks all present, then corrects exceptions.
3. System computes and stores present/absent/leave/late counts.
4. For each student absent without leave or sick-bay status, system alerts the parent and the dashboard.

#### Exception Paths
- If a second session is attempted for the same hostel/date/shift: the existing one is returned.
- After 24 hours: the session is locked and read-only.

#### Notifications Triggered
| At Step | Who Receives | Message Summary |
|---------|-------------|-----------------|
| 4 | Parent | Your child was marked absent at roll call. |

### 6.5 In-Out Movement & Overdue-Return Workflow
**Trigger:** A boarder leaves campus on a gate pass.
**End State:** Student logged back in, or flagged overdue with parent alerted.

#### Steps
1. Warden logs out-movement with destination and expected return time.
2. Student appears on the pending-returns list until they return.
3. On return, warden records the actual in-time.
4. Decision: If overdue beyond the threshold before returning → alert the parent once.

#### Exception Paths
- If the student returns much later than expected: the overdue alert has already fired; the return is still logged.

#### Notifications Triggered
| At Step | Who Receives | Message Summary |
|---------|-------------|-----------------|
| 4 | Parent | Your child is overdue returning to the hostel. |

### 6.6 Discipline Incident & Escalation Workflow
**Trigger:** A warden records an incident (or the system auto-creates a late-return incident).
**End State:** Incident recorded; parent notified for moderate/serious; escalated and/or warning letter issued where required.

#### Steps
1. Warden records the incident with type, severity, description and optional evidence.
2. Decision: If severity is moderate or serious → notify the parent automatically.
3. Decision: If escalation is warranted → escalate to the principal.
4. Optionally generate a warning letter (its content is then immutable).
5. System checks the student's incident count for the year.
   - Decision: If the threshold is reached → flag as repeated offender.

#### Exception Paths
- If the incident is system-generated (late return): it is recorded automatically and follows the same notify/escalate checks.

#### Notifications Triggered
| At Step | Who Receives | Message Summary |
|---------|-------------|-----------------|
| 2 | Parent | An incident involving your child has been recorded. |
| 3 | Principal | An incident has been escalated for review. |

### 6.7 Hostel Complaint SLA & Escalation Workflow
**Trigger:** A complaint/maintenance request is lodged.
**End State:** Complaint resolved with a note, or auto-escalated to the chief warden.

#### Steps
1. Requester lodges the complaint with category, description, room and optional photos.
2. Complaint is assigned to responsible staff and worked open → in progress.
3. Decision: To resolve → a resolution note is required.
4. A scheduled check runs periodically; if a complaint passes its service deadline unresolved → auto-escalate to the chief warden.

#### Exception Paths
- If no staff is assigned before the deadline: the complaint still escalates to the chief warden.

#### Notifications Triggered
| At Step | Who Receives | Message Summary |
|---------|-------------|-----------------|
| 4 | Chief Warden | A hostel complaint has breached its service deadline. |

### 6.8 Sick Bay Admission & Discharge Workflow
**Trigger:** Medical staff admit a boarder to the sick bay.
**End State:** Student discharged or referred to hospital; attendance restored.

#### Steps
1. Staff check sick-bay capacity and admit the student with symptoms/assessment.
   - Decision: If at capacity → block admission.
2. System auto-marks attendance as sick-bay for the admission period and notifies the parent.
3. Staff record vitals and medication as needed.
4. Decision: Discharge → record notes, resume normal attendance, notify the parent.
5. Decision: Hospital referral → set the referral flag, link to the health-records module, notify the parent.

#### Exception Paths
- If the sick bay is full: the student cannot be admitted until a bed frees up.

#### Notifications Triggered
| At Step | Who Receives | Message Summary |
|---------|-------------|-----------------|
| 2 | Parent | Your child has been admitted to the sick bay. |
| 4/5 | Parent | Your child has been discharged / referred to hospital. |

### 6.9 Mess Opt-Out & Monthly Billing Workflow
**Trigger:** A boarder opts out of meals; month closes.
**End State:** Finalised mess bill handed to the fee system.

#### Steps
1. Boarder/staff submits a meal opt-out before the cut-off.
   - Decision: If after cut-off → reject.
2. Through the month, meal attendance and leave-driven on-leave marks accumulate.
3. At month end, the system computes each bill (base + special diet − leave credit − opt-out credit + adjustment).
4. Supervisor/accountant finalises the bill (total not editable).
5. Finalised bill is pushed to the fee system.

#### Exception Paths
- If a bill is disputed: it can be adjusted via the manual-adjustment component, but the total stays formula-computed.

#### Notifications Triggered
| At Step | Who Receives | Message Summary |
|---------|-------------|-----------------|
| 5 | Accountant / Parent | The monthly mess bill is finalised. |

### 6.10 Room Reservation → Conversion Workflow
**Trigger:** A bed is reserved for a prospective student during admission.
**End State:** Reservation converted to an allotment, or expired and released.

#### Steps
1. Staff reserve a bed capturing the prospective student's name/contact.
2. The bed is held for the configured hold period.
3. Decision: On confirmation, if the student record exists and the bed is free → convert to an active allotment.
4. Decision: If the hold period lapses unconfirmed → expire the reservation and release the bed.

#### Exception Paths
- If the held bed is needed before conversion: an admin can cancel the reservation, releasing the bed.

#### Notifications Triggered
| At Step | Who Receives | Message Summary |
|---------|-------------|-----------------|
| 3 | Admission staff | Reservation confirmed and converted to an allotment. |

| Data isolation | One school's hostel data is never visible to another school |
| Audit trail | Allotment, leave, incident and bulk-vacate changes record who made them and when, and cannot be edited |
| Sensitive data | Medical (sick bay), conduct (incidents) and visitor ID-proof data are restricted to authorised roles; ID-proof numbers are masked |
| Notification delivery | The system logs whether each safety alert was queued, sent, delivered or failed |

### 9.3 Usability Requirements
| Requirement | Standard |
|-------------|---------|
| Mobile access | Roll-call, leave approval, movement and incident screens must work on mobile browsers for wardens on the move |
| Bulk efficiency | Attendance and mess marking support mark-all-then-correct for large hostels |
| Language | All labels and messages in English (Hindi/regional as a future enhancement) |
| Printable documents | Leave/gate pass, visitor pass and warning letters are printable in a clean PDF format |
| Empty states | Lists (pending returns, open incidents, defaulters) show a clear "nothing pending" message when empty |
