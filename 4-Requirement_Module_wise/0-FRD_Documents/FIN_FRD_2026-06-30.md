## Section 1: Module Overview

### 1.1 Purpose

The StudentFee module is the core financial operations engine for Prime-AI tenant schools. It manages the complete fee lifecycle — from configuring fee components and installment schedules, through bulk-assigning fees to students, generating invoices, collecting payments (cash, cheque, or online), issuing receipts, processing concessions and scholarships, applying late-payment fines, handling refunds, and producing financial reports and dashboards. The module serves the Accountant, School Admin, and Principal in their daily financial operations, and provides fee visibility to Class Teachers, Students, and Parents via the Student Portal.

### 1.2 Business Value

- Replaces manual fee registers and spreadsheets with a structured, auditable digital workflow
- Eliminates fee-structure duplication: one template per class-session-category combination, bulk-applied to all students
- Automates late-payment fine calculation nightly, removing manual penalty tracking
- Provides real-time outstanding-dues visibility to administrators and parents simultaneously
- Connects fee collection to the Accounting module for double-entry receipt voucher creation (D21 contract)
- Produces head-wise fee breakdowns required for Indian school statutory compliance (GST, 80G, RTE quota)

### 1.3 Scope

**In Scope:**
- Fee component (head) and group configuration
- Class-wise, session-wise, category-wise fee structure templates and installment schedules
- Individual and bulk student fee assignment
- Invoice generation (single and bulk), PDF download, email and WhatsApp delivery
- Offline payment recording (Cash, Cheque, Demand Draft, UPI, Card, Net Banking, Wallet)
- Online payment initiation and callback processing via Razorpay gateway
- Payment receipt generation and download
- Concession type configuration and per-student concession application with approval workflow
- Scholarship fund definition and student application-to-disbursement lifecycle
- Late-payment fine rule configuration and nightly automated fine application
- Fine waiver (full and partial) with reason and approver audit trail
- Cheque and Demand Draft clearance lifecycle (pending deposit → deposited → cleared / bounced → resubmitted)
- Fee refund initiation and approval workflow
- Student name removal due to persistent non-payment, and re-admission recording
- Fee dashboard with collection totals, defaulter list, and month-wise chart
- Fee reports: collection summary, defaulter list, concession list, scholarship list
- Annual fee structure rollover to the next academic session

**Out of Scope:**
- Staff salary and payroll (covered by HrStaff module)
- Hostel fee and mess billing (covered by Hostel module)
- Transport fee assignment (Transport module assigns transport-specific fees; FIN only handles collection once assigned)
- Library fines (Library module handles book-return fines independently)
- Double-entry accounting ledger maintenance (Accounting module; FIN emits an event that Accounting processes)
- Admission fee collection during the enquiry/application stage (Admission module handles pre-enrolment fee collection)

### 1.4 Terminology

| Business Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Fee Head | A single, named fee component that can be charged to students — for example, Tuition, Development, Examination, Library, or Sports. Each head has a frequency (monthly, quarterly, annual, or one-time), an optional tax percentage, and an accounting code that maps it to the school's ledger. |
| Fee Group | A logical bundle of one or more Fee Heads, grouped for convenience — for example, an "Academic Package" may bundle Tuition and Library heads. Groups can be marked as mandatory or optional for assignment. |
| Fee Structure | A class-specific, session-specific, and optionally category-specific template that defines the amount for each Fee Head applicable to students of that class in that academic session. One Fee Structure exists per class per session per student category. |
| Installment Schedule | A set of due dates dividing the academic-year fee into time-based portions (for example, three terms). Each installment specifies the percentage of the total fee due and a grace period (number of days beyond the due date before a fine is applied). |
| Student Fee Assignment | The record linking a specific student to a specific Fee Structure for an academic session. One assignment per student per session. It stores which optional heads and groups the student has opted into and handles mid-year join proration. |
| Fee Invoice | A billing document generated for a student against a specific installment. The invoice states the base amount, applied concessions, applicable fines, tax, and the total due. It has a six-step lifecycle: Draft, Published, Partially Paid, Paid, Overdue, Cancelled. |
| Payment Transaction | A record of money received against an invoice — partial or full. Each transaction records the payment mode, amount, reference number, and the person who collected it. |
| Payment Receipt | The official acknowledgement issued after a successful Payment Transaction. It carries a unique receipt number and can be downloaded as a PDF. |
| Concession | A discount applied to a student's fee, categorised by type (Sibling, Merit, Staff Ward, Financial Aid, Sports, Alumni). A concession may require approval before it reduces the student's invoice amount. |
| Scholarship | A fund-backed financial aid awarded to eligible students through an application-to-disbursement workflow. Disbursed scholarship amounts are applied as credits against the student's fee assignment, reducing future invoices. |
| Fine / Late-Payment Penalty | A monetary charge applied to a student's invoice after the installment's due date plus grace period has passed. The system applies fines automatically each night via a scheduled task. Fines can be configured as a per-day rate, a flat bracket amount, or a percentage of the base amount capped at a maximum. |
| Fine Waiver | Administrative cancellation or reduction of an applied fine, recorded with the approver's identity, reason, and the waived amount. |
| Defaulter | A student with one or more invoices in the Overdue status. The system maintains a defaulter analytics record per student per session, tracking the count and average days of overdue payments. |
| Name Removal | An escalation action triggered when persistent non-payment meets a configured threshold — the student's name is removed from the class register. The record captures the removal date, total outstanding amount, and re-admission details when the student returns. |
| Fee Rollover | An administrative action that copies Fee Structures from the current academic session to the next, with an optional uniform amount-adjustment percentage, to avoid recreating structures at the start of each year. |
| D21 Event Contract | The formal agreement between the StudentFee and Accounting modules: when a fee payment is successfully recorded, StudentFee emits a `FeePaymentReceived` event that the Accounting module consumes to create a Receipt Voucher in the double-entry ledger. |
| RTE Student | A student admitted under the Right to Education quota who may receive a zero-amount or heavily subsidised fee invoice without error from the billing system. |
| Proration | A proportional reduction of the annual fee for a student who joins mid-year, calculated as a percentage of the remaining months in the academic session. |
| Account Head Code | A short code on each Fee Head that maps it to the school's Chart of Accounts in the Accounting module, enabling automated voucher line-item creation. |

## Section 3: Functional Requirements

### REQ-FIN-001: Fee Head Configuration
**Priority:** Core (P0) | **Tags:** [CONFIGURATION][DATA_ENTRY]
**Source:** V2 FR-FIN-01; V1 fee-heads.md; module knowledge (FeeHeadMaster, FeeHeadMasterController)

**Description:** The School Admin can define the catalogue of fee components (heads) available to the school. Each component carries a name, a short code, a classification type (for example, Tuition or Library), a billing frequency, optional tax configuration, a refundability flag, and an accounting code for ledger integration. Components can be reordered, deactivated, or soft-deleted.

**Actors:** Initiates — School Admin | Processes — System | Views — School Admin, Accountant

**Business Rules Applied:**

| BR Reference | Rule |
|---|---|
| BR-FIN-001 | Each Fee Head code must be unique across all heads in the school's database; once assigned, it cannot be changed |
| BR-FIN-002 | When tax is enabled for a head, the tax amount for any invoice line is calculated as: Head Amount × Tax Percentage ÷ 100 |
| BR-FIN-003 | A Fee Head that is inactive is hidden from fee structure configuration dropdowns |
| BR-FIN-004 | A Fee Head linked to an active fee structure detail cannot be force-deleted |

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. A School Admin can create a Fee Head with a unique code (max 30 characters), name (max 100 characters), classification type, billing frequency, and optional tax percentage. YES/NO testable.
2. The system prevents saving a Fee Head with a duplicate code. YES/NO testable.
3. The code field becomes read-only after creation. YES/NO testable.
4. Deactivating a Fee Head removes it from fee structure dropdowns without deleting it. YES/NO testable.
5. A soft-deleted Fee Head can be restored; a force-deleted Head is permanently removed only if no structure details reference it. YES/NO testable.
6. Tax amount is correctly calculated as Head Amount × Tax% ÷ 100 when tax is enabled. YES/NO testable.

**Integration Points:** Accounting module reads `account_head_code` to map fee collection to ledger accounts (D21 contract).

**Enhancement Notes:** ENH-FIN-001 — Bulk import of Fee Heads via CSV for schools migrating from paper records.

### REQ-FIN-003: Fee Structure Definition
**Priority:** Core (P0) | **Tags:** [CONFIGURATION][DATA_ENTRY]
**Source:** V2 FR-FIN-03; V1 fee-structures.md

**Description:** The School Admin defines a Fee Structure for a specific academic session, class, and optional student category (General, OBC, SC/ST) or board type (CBSE, ICSE, State). The structure specifies the amount for each applicable Fee Head, whether the head is optional, and whether it includes tax. |---|---|

| BR-FIN-009 | The structure's total fee amount equals the sum of all non-optional head amounts in the structure detail |
| BR-FIN-010 | A Fee Structure cannot be soft-deleted if any Student Fee Assignment is linked to it |
| BR-FIN-011 | A Fee Structure effective date range must fall within the academic session dates |

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Creating a second structure for the same session + class + category is rejected by the system. YES/NO testable.
2. The system calculates and displays the total fee amount as the sum of mandatory head amounts. YES/NO testable.
3. Attempting to delete a structure with active student assignments returns a validation error. YES/NO testable.
4. Optional heads are excluded from the total fee amount but are stored in the structure detail. YES/NO testable.

**Integration Points:** Student Fee Assignment (REQ-FIN-005) selects a structure; Installment Schedule Management (REQ-FIN-004) associates schedules with a structure.

### REQ-FIN-005: Student Fee Assignment
**Priority:** Core (P0) | **Tags:** [WORKFLOW][DATA_ENTRY]
**Source:** V2 FR-FIN-05; V1 fee-assignments.md

**Description:** The School Admin or Accountant assigns a Fee Structure to students for the current academic session. Bulk assignment creates assignments for all active students in a class or section in one operation. Individual assignment allows selection of optional heads and groups. Mid-year joiners receive a prorated fee amount based on the number of months remaining in the session. Bulk assignment is idempotent — students already assigned are skipped without error.

**Actors:** Initiates — School Admin, Accountant | Processes — System | Views — School Admin, Accountant, Class Teacher (own class)

**Business Rules Applied:**

| BR Reference | Rule |
|---|---|
| BR-FIN-015 | One active Student Fee Assignment is permitted per student per academic session |
| BR-FIN-016 | A student's fee structure can be changed mid-year; doing so recalculates the total fee amount and requires review of any existing invoices |
| BR-FIN-017 | Mid-year proration percentage must be between 0 and 100; the fee start date must fall within the academic session dates |
| BR-FIN-018 | Bulk assignment runs are idempotent — students with existing assignments for the same session are skipped |

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Bulk assignment creates an assignment record for every active student in the selected class or section who does not yet have one for the current session. YES/NO testable.
2. Running bulk assignment a second time does not create duplicate assignment records. YES/NO testable.
3. A student can have at most one active assignment per academic session; a second assignment creation attempt is rejected. YES/NO testable.
4. For a mid-year joiner with proration at 60%, the stored fee amount equals 60% of the structure total. YES/NO testable.
5. The section dropdown refreshes dynamically when a different class is selected. YES/NO testable.

**Integration Points:** StudentProfile module provides the list of active students and class-section membership. SchoolSetup provides academic session and class data.

### REQ-FIN-006: Fee Invoice Generation
**Priority:** Core (P0) | **Tags:** [WORKFLOW][DATA_ENTRY][NOTIFICATION]
**Source:** V2 FR-FIN-08; V1 fee-invoicing.md

**Description:** The School Admin or Accountant generates fee invoices for student assignments. Individual invoice generation targets a specific student assignment and installment. Bulk generation produces invoices for all active assignments in the session that do not yet have an invoice for the selected installment. Each invoice carries a uniquely auto-generated number (format: INV-YYYYMM-XXXX), the base amount, applied concessions, applicable fines, tax, and the total due. Invoices can be published to the Student Portal, downloaded as PDF, emailed, or shared via WhatsApp. Invoices can be cancelled by an authorised user with a mandatory reason.

**Actors:** Initiates — School Admin, Accountant | Processes — System | Views — School Admin, Accountant, Principal, Student (own), Parent (child)

**Business Rules Applied:**

| BR Reference | Rule |
|---|---|
| BR-FIN-019 | Invoice numbers are unique across all invoices in the school; the format is INV-YYYYMM-XXXX where XXXX is a per-month sequence |
| BR-FIN-020 | Only one invoice per student assignment per installment may exist in an active (non-cancelled) state |
| BR-FIN-021 | Invoice total amount = base amount − concession amount + fine amount + tax amount |
| BR-FIN-022 | The outstanding balance displayed on an invoice equals: total amount − paid amount |
| BR-FIN-023 | An invoice in the Paid status cannot be cancelled |
| BR-FIN-024 | A cancelled invoice is immutable — no payment can be recorded against it |
| BR-FIN-025 | Invoice status transitions: Draft → Published → Partially Paid → Paid → Overdue → Cancelled; an Overdue invoice becomes Paid when all outstanding amounts are settled |
| BR-FIN-026 | Bulk invoice generation is idempotent — students with an existing invoice for the same installment are skipped |

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. An invoice is generated with a unique INV-YYYYMM-XXXX number. YES/NO testable.
2. Bulk generation creates invoices only for assignments without one for the selected installment. YES/NO testable.
3. Invoice total equals base − concession + fine + tax. YES/NO testable.
4. Attempting to cancel a Paid invoice returns a validation error. YES/NO testable.
5. A cancelled invoice returns a validation error when a payment recording is attempted against it. YES/NO testable.
6. A PDF of the invoice can be downloaded by authorised users. YES/NO testable.
7. Emailing an invoice attaches the PDF and sends via the Notification channel. YES/NO testable.

**Integration Points:** Notification module dispatches invoice emails and WhatsApp links. Student Portal displays invoices to students and parents. Accounting module receives a voucher creation trigger on payment (see REQ-FIN-007).

**Enhancement Notes:** ENH-FIN-002 — Queue bulk generation for batches over 100 students to prevent HTTP timeouts.

### REQ-FIN-007: Offline Payment Recording
**Priority:** Core (P0) | **Tags:** [WORKFLOW][DATA_ENTRY]
**Source:** V2 FR-FIN-09; V1 fee-payments.md

**Description:** The Accountant or School Admin records a payment received against a specific invoice. The system accepts partial and full payments. Each payment record captures the mode (Cash, Cheque, Demand Draft, UPI, Credit Card, Debit Card, Net Banking, or Wallet), the amount, a reference number, bank details for cheque or DD, the collection date, and the person who collected it. After each payment, the invoice's paid amount is updated and its status is recalculated. A receipt is issued for every successful payment.

**Actors:** Initiates — School Admin, Accountant | Processes — System | Views — School Admin, Accountant, Principal, Student (own), Parent (child)

**Business Rules Applied:**

| BR Reference | Rule |
|---|---|
| BR-FIN-027 | A partial payment is allowed; the invoice status changes to Partially Paid when payment is greater than zero but less than the outstanding total |
| BR-FIN-028 | The invoice status changes to Paid when cumulative paid amount equals or exceeds the total amount |
| BR-FIN-029 | Overpayment (paid amount exceeds total amount) is accepted; excess is recorded as advance credit against the student's account for adjustment in the next invoice |
| BR-FIN-030 | After a partial payment, the late-payment fine continues to accumulate on the remaining outstanding balance; paying partially does not reset the due date |
| BR-FIN-031 | All payment write operations (invoice update and transaction creation) are executed inside a single database transaction; a failure in either step rolls back both |
| BR-FIN-032 | Successful payment transactions are immutable — they cannot be edited or deleted |
| BR-FIN-033 | A cheque or DD reference number and bank name are mandatory fields when the payment mode is Cheque or Demand Draft |
| BR-FIN-034 | All financial operations are logged in the system activity log with the causer's identity, the subject record, and before/after values |

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Recording a partial payment sets the invoice status to Partially Paid. YES/NO testable.
2. Recording the remaining balance of a Partially Paid invoice changes the status to Paid. YES/NO testable.
3. A cheque payment without a reference number is rejected. YES/NO testable.
4. If the transaction record creation fails after the invoice update, the invoice update is rolled back. YES/NO testable.
5. A receipt is generated and can be downloaded for every successful payment. YES/NO testable.
6. A paid transaction record cannot be modified or deleted. YES/NO testable.

**Integration Points:** Accounting module receives a `FeePaymentReceived` event to create a receipt voucher (D21 contract). Cheque payments create a corresponding Cheque Clearance Record (REQ-FIN-015). Online payment is handled in REQ-FIN-008.

### REQ-FIN-008: Online Payment via Razorpay Gateway
**Priority:** Standard (P1) | **Tags:** [WORKFLOW][INTEGRATION]
**Source:** V2 FR-FIN-09.7; Section 6.4 planned API; module knowledge payment-gateway section

**Description:** Students and Parents can initiate online payment for an invoice through the Student Portal or Parent Portal. The system creates a Razorpay payment order, redirects the user to the Razorpay checkout, and processes the payment callback. On successful payment, the invoice is updated and a receipt is generated — identical to an offline payment. The gateway log records the full request and response payload for audit purposes.

**Actors:** Initiates — Student, Parent | Processes — System, Razorpay | Views — Student, Parent, Accountant, School Admin

**Business Rules Applied:**

| BR Reference | Rule |
|---|---|
| BR-FIN-035 | The Razorpay payment callback endpoint must verify the HMAC-SHA256 signature (`X-Razorpay-Signature` header) before processing; unsigned or invalid callbacks are rejected with a 400 response |
| BR-FIN-036 | The callback handler must check that the Razorpay `payment_id` has not already been processed (idempotency guard) before updating the invoice |
| BR-FIN-037 | The callback route must not require user authentication (Razorpay posts server-to-server); it must be excluded from CSRF verification |
| BR-FIN-038 | The amount sent to the Razorpay order creation API must be the server-computed outstanding balance, not a client-supplied value |
| BR-FIN-039 | Gateway log records (request, response payloads, transaction ID, order ID, payment ID) are treated as financially sensitive data |

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. A student initiating online payment is redirected to a Razorpay checkout for the correct outstanding balance amount. YES/NO testable.
2. A valid Razorpay callback with correct signature updates the invoice status and creates a receipt. YES/NO testable.
3. A callback with an invalid or missing signature returns a 400 error and makes no data changes. YES/NO testable.
4. A replayed callback with a previously processed `payment_id` is ignored without creating a duplicate payment record. YES/NO testable.
5. Tampered client-side amounts are ignored; the server uses only the amount on the invoice record. YES/NO testable.

**Integration Points:** Payment module (PAY) provides Razorpay integration infrastructure. The `FeeInvoice` model implements the `Payable` contract.

### REQ-FIN-010: Concession Management
**Priority:** Core (P0) | **Tags:** [WORKFLOW][APPROVAL]
**Source:** V2 FR-FIN-06; V1 fee-concessions.md

**Description:** The School Admin configures concession types (e.g., Sibling Discount, Merit Scholarship, Staff Ward, Financial Hardship). Each concession type specifies the discount method (percentage or fixed amount), the maximum cap, whether approval is required, and which fee heads or groups the discount applies to. Staff then apply a concession type to a specific student's assignment. If the concession type requires approval, it enters a Pending state and a notification is sent to the designated approver (Principal or School Admin). Approved concessions are automatically included in the next invoice generated for that student. Multiple concessions per student are allowed and cumulative, subject to individual cap limits.

**Actors:** Initiates (apply) — School Admin, Accountant | Approves — Principal, School Admin | Processes — System | Views — School Admin, Accountant, Principal

**Business Rules Applied:**

| BR Reference | Rule |
|---|---|
| BR-FIN-043 | A concession requiring approval is not applied to invoice calculations until its status is Approved |
| BR-FIN-044 | Multiple approved concessions for the same student are cumulative; each concession is capped individually at its configured maximum amount |
| BR-FIN-045 | Sibling discount eligibility: two or more students sharing the same fee-paying guardian in the same academic session |
| BR-FIN-046 | Merit concession eligibility: verified manually by the admin; no automatic check against exam results in the current version |
| BR-FIN-047 | Staff Ward concession: requires an active staff record linked to the student's guardian |
| BR-FIN-048 | When a concession requiring approval is submitted, a notification is sent to the approver designated in the concession type configuration |
| BR-FIN-049 | A rejection must include a mandatory rejection reason stored with the record |
| BR-FIN-050 | Concession applicable to specific heads or groups: the check constraint ensures each mapping row targets exactly one head or one group, not both |

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. A concession type can be configured as percentage or fixed amount with an optional maximum cap. YES/NO testable.
2. Applying a concession marked as requiring approval sets its status to Pending and sends a notification to the approver. YES/NO testable.
3. A Pending concession amount does not appear in the invoice concession amount. YES/NO testable.
4. Approving a concession changes its status to Approved; the next invoice generated for the student reflects the discount. YES/NO testable.
5. Rejecting a concession requires a rejection reason and changes status to Rejected. YES/NO testable.
6. Two approved concessions are added cumulatively in the invoice, each checked against its individual cap. YES/NO testable.

**Integration Points:** Notification module dispatches concession approval request to the approver. Invoice Generation (REQ-FIN-006) reads approved concessions when computing invoice amounts.

### REQ-FIN-011: Scholarship Management
**Priority:** Standard (P1) | **Tags:** [WORKFLOW][APPROVAL]
**Source:** V2 FR-FIN-07; V1 fee-scholarships.md

### REQ-FIN-013: Automated Fine Application
**Priority:** Core (P0) | **Tags:** [SCHEDULED][NOTIFICATION]
**Source:** V2 FR-FIN-10.5, FR-FIN-10.8; V1 fee-fines.md

**Description:** The system runs a scheduled task nightly that identifies all Published, Partially Paid, and Overdue invoices whose installment due date plus grace period has passed. | BR-FIN-065 | The nightly task failure for one invoice must not abort processing of subsequent invoices; errors are collected and reported separately |

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Running the fine task on a school with one overdue invoice (3 days past grace period, Per-Day rule at Rs. 10) creates exactly one fine record with Rs. 30. YES/NO testable.

### 5.2 Key Entity Relationships (Business Narrative)

- A **Fee Head** can belong to multiple **Fee Groups** and appears in multiple **Fee Structures**.
- A **Fee Structure** is composed of **Fee Structure Details** (one per applicable head) and has one or more **Installment Schedules**.
- A **Student Fee Assignment** links one student to one Fee Structure for one academic session.
- A **Fee Invoice** is generated from a Student Fee Assignment for one Installment.
- **Payment Transactions** are recorded against Fee Invoices; each transaction produces one **Payment Receipt**.
- **Fine Records** are associated with overdue Fee Invoices.
- **Student Concessions** are applied at the Assignment level and reduce invoice amounts.
- **Scholarship Applications** are submitted per student per fund; disbursed amounts reduce the student's assignment totals.
- **Cheque Clearance Records** are linked one-to-one with cheque/DD Payment Transactions.
- **Refund Requests** are linked to the original Payment Transaction.
- **Name Removal Records** are linked to a student and academic session.

### 5.3 Multi-Tenancy and Data Isolation

All 24 data entities live in the tenant's isolated database (`tenant_{uuid}`). There is no `school_id` or `tenant_id` column on any table. Isolation is enforced entirely by the database connection switch at the application layer when a request arrives for a school's domain. No data from one school can be visible to another school. This rule applies to every query in the StudentFee module without exception.

### 5.4 Privacy Classification Notes

- **Sensitive:** Payment Gateway Logs contain financial transaction references (Razorpay payment IDs, order IDs) and raw request/response payloads. These fields must not be included in general-purpose data exports.
- **Confidential:** All student-linked financial records (invoices, transactions, receipts, concessions, defaulter history) must be accessible only to users with the correct role permission and must never be exposed to users of other classes or year groups without explicit role authorization.
- **Public:** Fee Head and Fee Group names, Scholarship Fund names (not eligibility criteria JSON), and Fine Rule names may appear in student-facing UIs.

## Section 6: Workflows

### Workflow 1: Offline Payment Collection
**Trigger:** Accountant or School Admin opens a fee invoice and clicks "Record Payment"
**End States:** Invoice status = Paid (if full payment) or Partially Paid (if partial)
**Actors:** Accountant / System

**Steps:**
1. Accountant opens the invoice and confirms the outstanding balance.
2. Accountant selects payment mode, enters amount, reference details, and collection date.
3. System validates: amount ≤ outstanding balance (allows partial); reference mandatory for cheque/DD.
4. System writes the payment transaction and updates the invoice's paid amount inside a single database transaction.
5. System recalculates invoice status: ≥ total → Paid; < total → Partially Paid.
6. System generates a receipt and makes it downloadable.
7. If payment mode is Cheque or DD: system auto-creates a Cheque Clearance Record in Pending Deposit state.
8. System emits a `FeePaymentReceived` event; Accounting module creates a Receipt Voucher.
9. System logs the transaction to the activity log.

**Exception Paths:**
- If database write fails: both transaction and invoice update are rolled back; user sees an error.
- If overpayment: invoice status → Paid; excess recorded as advance credit.
- If invoice is already Cancelled: the "Record Payment" button is hidden; direct URL access returns a 403 error.

**Notifications Triggered:**

| Step | Recipient | Channel | Message |
|---|---|---|---|
| Step 6 | Parent / Guardian | Email, WhatsApp (optional) | "Payment of ₹{amount} received for {student name}. Receipt #{receipt_no} attached." |

---

### Workflow 2: Concession Approval
**Trigger:** Staff submits a concession application for a student's fee assignment
**End States:** Concession status = Approved (applied to invoice) or Rejected (reason stored)
**Actors:** School Admin / Accountant (submitter); Principal / School Admin (approver)

**Steps:**
1. Staff opens the student's fee assignment and selects a concession type.
2. System checks whether the concession type requires approval.
   - **No approval required:** Concession status → Applied immediately; invoice amounts recalculated on next generation.
   - **Approval required:** Proceed to step 3.
3. System creates the concession record with status Pending.
4. System dispatches a notification to the role designated as the approver in the concession type configuration.
5. Approver views the pending concession and selects Approve or Reject.
6. On Approve: status → Approved; concession amount is included in the next invoice generated for the student.
7. On Reject: status → Rejected; mandatory rejection reason stored.

**Exception Paths:**
- Approver is inactive or role unassigned: notification cannot be delivered; admin sees a warning that the approver role has no active users.
- Student already has a Paid invoice for the session: concession applies only to future or unpaid invoices; admin is warned.

**Notifications Triggered:**

| Step | Recipient | Channel | Message |
|---|---|---|---|
| Step 4 | Designated Approver | In-app + Email | "A {concession type} concession for {student name} requires your approval." |
| Step 6 | Submitting Staff | In-app | "Concession for {student name} has been approved by {approver name}." |
| Step 7 | Submitting Staff | In-app | "Concession for {student name} was rejected. Reason: {rejection reason}." |

---

### Workflow 3: Scholarship Application to Disbursement
**Trigger:** Student or staff member submits a scholarship application
**End States:** Disbursed (scholarship amount applied to student fee) or Rejected / Waitlisted
**Actors:** Student / School Admin (submitter); Principal / School Admin (reviewer and approver); System (fund balance management)

**Steps:**
1. Applicant creates a draft application, attaches supporting documents, and submits.
2. Status → Submitted.
3. Admin marks the application Under Review.
4. Admin (or Principal) selects Approve, Reject, or Waitlist.
5. On Approval: status → Approved; approved amount recorded.
6. Admin triggers Disbursement.
7. System checks: available fund balance ≥ approved amount. If insufficient: blocked with error.
8. System decrements the fund's available balance, sets the disbursed flag, and applies the amount as a credit to the student's fee assignment.
9. Approval history record created at every stage transition.

**Exception Paths:**
- Application submitted after the application end date: blocked at submission (Step 2 fails with validation error).
- Duplicate application (same student, same fund, same session): blocked at step 1 with validation error.
- Fund exhausted before disbursement: disbursement blocked (step 7 fails).

---

### Workflow 4: Nightly Fine Application (Automated)
**Trigger:** System scheduler at 00:30 daily
**End States:** Overdue invoices have fresh fine records; defaulter analytics updated; name removal records created where applicable
**Actors:** System (scheduler)

**Steps:**
1. Scheduler fires the `ApplyFines` command.
2. Command fetches all Published, Partially Paid, and Overdue invoices whose installment due date + grace days < today.
3. For each invoice:
   a. Loads applicable fine rules.
   b. Checks whether a fine record already exists for today (idempotency guard); if yes, skip.
   c. Calculates fine amount using the applicable mode (Per-Day, Flat Per Tier, or Percentage-Capped).
   d. If calculated fine + cumulative fine ≥ max fine amount: apply only the remaining allowed amount.
   e. Creates a Fine Record and updates the invoice's fine amount.
   f. Updates invoice status to Overdue if not already.
   g. If action_on_expiry = "Mark Defaulter": upserts the student's Defaulter Analytics Record.
   h. If action_on_expiry = "Remove Name": creates a Name Removal Record; notifies principal and admin.
4. Collects any per-invoice errors; outputs a summary report (success count, error count, error details).

**Exception Paths:**
- Fine task fails for one invoice: error recorded; subsequent invoices continue processing.
- Database connection failure mid-run: task restarts cleanly on next trigger; idempotency guard prevents duplication.

**Notifications Triggered:**

| Step | Recipient | Channel | Message |
|---|---|---|---|
| Step 3e | Parent / Guardian | SMS / Email | "A late-payment fine of ₹{fine_amount} has been applied to {student name}'s invoice #{invoice_no}." |
| Step 3h | Principal, School Admin | In-app + Email | "Student {student name} (Class {class}) has been removed from the class register due to non-payment." |

---

### Workflow 5: Cheque Clearance
**Trigger:** A payment is recorded with mode = Cheque or Demand Draft
**End States:** Clearance status = Cleared (payment confirmed) or Resubmitted (after bounce)
**Actors:** Accountant (clearance management); System (invoice reversal on bounce)

**Steps:**
1. Cheque payment is recorded in REQ-FIN-007 → Clearance Record auto-created with status Pending Deposit.
2. Accountant marks the cheque as deposited when it is physically sent to the bank (status → Deposited).
3. Bank confirmation received:
   - **Cleared:** Accountant marks the record as Cleared (status → Cleared). Invoice remains Paid/Partially Paid.
   - **Bounced:** Accountant records the bounce reason and bounce charge (status → Bounced).
4. On bounce:
   a. System reverses the linked invoice: paid amount reduced by the bounced transaction amount; invoice status recalculated.
   b. System creates a bounce charge Fine Record.
   c. System notifies the parent or guardian.
5. Accountant records the resubmission date (status → Resubmitted); the cheque re-enters the Deposited flow.
