## Section 1 — Module Overview

### 1.1 Purpose

The Vendor module enables a school to manage the complete lifecycle of its supplier relationships. From onboarding a new supplier, recording a formal agreement with billing terms, tracking actual service consumption, generating GST-compliant invoices, and recording payments — this module replaces manual spreadsheet-based procurement tracking with a structured, auditable digital workflow.

This module is the school's Accounts Payable foundation, covering all money owed to external suppliers for goods and services. It supports three billing models (flat-rate, consumption-based, and hybrid) to match how different vendor relationships are structured in practice.

### 1.2 Business Value

- Eliminates manual invoice calculation errors through an automated billing engine
- Provides audit-ready records of every vendor transaction with user attribution
- Ensures GST compliance with GSTIN/PAN validation and HSN/SAC code capture on all items
- Gives finance managers real-time visibility into outstanding payments, expiring contracts, and payment health
- Supports future integration with the Accounting module for double-entry journal voucher posting

### 1.3 Scope

**In Scope:**
- Vendor master registration: identity, tax details (GSTIN/PAN), bank details, documents
- Item and service catalogue: goods and services the school procures, with HSN/SAC codes
- Agreement (contract) management: terms, billing cycle, payment period, status lifecycle
- Agreement line items: per-item billing model, pricing, tax configuration, linked asset
- Usage logging: tracking actual consumption for per-unit and hybrid billing
- Invoice generation: single and batch; three billing model calculation engine
- Invoice actions: PDF generation, ZIP batch download, email dispatch to vendor
- Payment recording: multi-payment per invoice, payment modes, reconciliation
- Vendor dashboard: real-time financial KPIs and expiry alerts
- Five standard reports: Vendor Ledger Summary, Agreement Report, Invoice Register, Outstanding Report, Payment Register
- Agreement auto-expiry: scheduled daily transition of contracts past their end date
- Document management: vendor KYC documents, signed agreement PDFs, item photos

**Out of Scope (current release):**
- Full Accounts Payable ledger posting — requires the Accounting module (ACC) to be built first
- Purchase Order (PO) management — specified for a future release
- Goods Receipt Note (GRN) and three-way matching (PO–GRN–Invoice) — requires Inventory module (INV)
- Invoice approval workflow (approver gate before payment) — future enhancement
- TDS deduction tracking and quarterly Form 26Q reporting — future enhancement
- Vendor self-service portal (vendor-initiated invoice submission via secure token) — future
- Vendor performance scorecard (delivery and quality ratings) — future
- GSTR filing integration — Accounting module scope

### 1.4 Terminology

| Business Term | Definition |
|---------------|-----------|
| Vendor | A third-party company or individual that supplies goods or services to the school |
| GSTIN | Goods and Services Tax Identification Number — 15-character tax identifier issued to GST-registered businesses in India |
| PAN | Permanent Account Number — 10-character income tax identifier for businesses and individuals in India |
| Agreement | A formal contract between the school and a vendor, defining what is supplied, for how long, at what price, and under which billing model |
| Agreement Item | A single line within an agreement describing one specific good or service, its billing model, rates, and tax structure |
| Billing Model | The method used to calculate the invoice amount: Fixed (flat rate), Per Unit (usage-based), or Hybrid (fixed base plus per-unit overage) |
| Minimum Guarantee Quantity | In Hybrid billing, the quantity included in the fixed base charge; usage above this is billed at the per-unit rate |
| Usage Log | A record of actual service or product consumption for a given agreement item on a specific date |
| Balance Due | The remaining unpaid amount on an invoice, calculated as Net Payable minus Amount Already Paid |
| Reconciliation | The process of matching a recorded payment to a bank statement transaction to confirm the money has cleared |
| HSN Code | Harmonised System of Nomenclature code — tax classification for goods under GST |
| SAC Code | Service Accounting Code — tax classification for services under GST |
| Invoice Status | The current payment state of an invoice: Pending, Partially Paid, Fully Paid (Overdue in future release) |
| Billing Cycle | How frequently the school expects to be billed: Monthly, One-Time, or On-Demand |
| Payment Terms | The number of days from invoice date within which the school must pay; determines the invoice due date |

### REQ-VND-001: Vendor Registration and Profile Management
**Priority:** Core (P0) | **Tags:** [DATA_ENTRY][CONFIGURATION]

**Description:**
A Finance Manager or School Admin can register a new vendor by capturing identity information, tax credentials, banking details, and optionally uploading vendor documents. Each vendor record is active by default and can be toggled inactive, soft-deleted, or permanently removed.

**Actors:** Initiates: Finance Manager, School Admin | Processes: System (validation, document storage) | Views: Finance Manager, Accountant, Purchase Manager, General Staff, Transport Manager

**Business Rules:**

| BR Ref | Rule |
|--------|------|
| BR-VND-001 | GSTIN must be unique per school — no two vendors may share the same 15-character GSTIN |
| BR-VND-001 | PAN must be unique per school — no two vendors may share the same 10-character PAN |
| BR-VND-012 | A vendor with active agreements or pending invoices must display a warning before soft-delete is permitted |
| BR-VND-019 | Only active vendors appear in agreement creation dropdowns |

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. A Finance Manager who submits a new vendor with all required fields receives a success confirmation and the vendor appears in the vendor list.
2. A vendor submitted with a GSTIN in the wrong format (not 15 characters, wrong pattern) is rejected with a format-specific validation message.
3. A vendor submitted with a GSTIN already used by another vendor in the same school is rejected with a uniqueness error.
4. An admin who toggles a vendor to Inactive sees that vendor absent from agreement creation dropdowns and invoice generation vendor filters.
5. An admin who soft-deletes a vendor with active agreements receives a warning message before the deletion proceeds.
6. A user without the "Create Vendor" permission who accesses the create form receives an access-denied response.
7. Vendor data (name, contact, bank details) from one school is never visible to users of a different school.

**Integrations:** Document uploads via the media library (vendor documents collection). Bank details referenced by Finance Manager during payment processing. Vendor type dropdown drives Transport module filtering.

**Enhancement Notes:** Future: encrypt PAN and bank account number at rest; add GSTIN lookup against GST portal API for auto-fill.

### REQ-VND-003: Agreement (Contract) Management
**Priority:** Core (P0) | **Tags:** [DATA_ENTRY][WORKFLOW][CONFIGURATION]

**Description:**
A Finance Manager or School Admin can record the formal contract between the school and a vendor. An agreement captures the contract period, billing cycle, payment terms, and status. Agreements progress through a defined status lifecycle: Draft — Active — Expired/Terminated.

**Actors:** Initiates: Finance Manager, School Admin | Processes: System (status enforcement, auto-expiry) | Views: Finance Manager, Accountant, Purchase Manager

**Business Rules:**

| BR Ref | Rule |
|--------|------|
| BR-VND-006 | Agreement end date must be after start date |
| BR-VND-011 | An agreement cannot be moved from Draft to Active unless it has at least one active agreement item |
| BR-VND-007 | A daily automated process sets any Active agreement whose end date has passed to Expired |
| BR-VND-019 | Only active vendors can be selected when creating an agreement |

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. A Finance Manager who creates an agreement with a valid vendor, reference number, date range, billing cycle, and payment terms sees the agreement saved in Draft status.
2. An agreement submitted with end date before start date is rejected with a date-range validation error.
3. An admin who attempts to activate a Draft agreement with no agreement items receives an error: "At least one agreement item is required before activation."
4. An agreement in Active status whose end date was yesterday appears as Expired the following morning after the scheduled process runs.
5. A Finance Manager who manually terminates an Active agreement sees its status change to Terminated immediately.
6. A signed agreement document (PDF) can be uploaded and the "document uploaded" flag is set to Yes on the agreement list.
7. An Expired or Terminated agreement does not appear in the invoice generation agreement item list.

**Integrations:** Agreement reference number appears on all invoices generated under it. Expiry notification sent to Finance Manager via Notification module when daily job runs.

### REQ-VND-004: Agreement Items with Billing Models
**Priority:** Core (P0) | **Tags:** [DATA_ENTRY][CONFIGURATION]

**Description:**
Each agreement contains one or more line items specifying what is billed, at what rate, and under which billing model. Three billing models are supported. Each line item can optionally be linked to a specific school asset (vehicle, driver, building asset) for contract traceability.

**Actors:** Initiates: Finance Manager, School Admin | Views: Finance Manager, Accountant, Purchase Manager

**Business Rules:**

| BR Ref | Rule |
|--------|------|
| BR-VND-008 | A Monthly agreement may have only one generated invoice per agreement item per calendar month |
| BR-VND-008 | A One-Time agreement may have only one generated invoice per agreement item for the lifetime of the agreement |
| BR-VND-020 | Only active catalogue items can be added to an agreement |

**Billing Model Calculations (at invoice generation time):**
- **Fixed:** Sub-total equals the fixed charge amount. Usage quantity has no effect on the amount.
- **Per Unit:** Sub-total equals unit rate multiplied by total quantity used (aggregated from usage logs for the billing period).
- **Hybrid:** Sub-total equals fixed charge plus (unit rate multiplied by the excess of quantity used over the minimum guarantee quantity, with a floor of zero).

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. A Finance Manager who adds a Fixed billing item to an agreement sees a form requiring fixed charge, tax percentages, and optional asset linkage.
2. A Finance Manager who adds a Per Unit item sees additional fields for unit rate.
3. A Finance Manager who adds a Hybrid item sees fields for fixed charge, unit rate, and minimum guarantee quantity.
4. An agreement item can be linked to a specific vehicle or asset; the linked asset name is displayed on the agreement item list.
5. A tax-exempt item (all four tax percentages set to zero) calculates net payable equal to sub-total with no tax addition.

### REQ-VND-005: Usage Logging
**Priority:** Core (P0) | **Tags:** [DATA_ENTRY]

**Description:**
For Per Unit and Hybrid billing, the school tracks actual service consumption or product delivery through usage log entries. Each entry records a vendor, agreement item, date, and quantity. The total logged quantity is consumed by the invoice generation engine at billing time.

**Actors:** Initiates: Finance Manager, Purchase Manager | Views: Finance Manager, Accountant, Purchase Manager

**Business Rules:**

| BR Ref | Rule |
|--------|------|
| BR-VND-013 | Quantity used must be greater than zero |
| BR-VND-014 | Usage date should fall within the agreement period |
| BR-VND-015 | If no usage has been logged for an agreement item when invoice generation is triggered, the system uses a default quantity of one to prevent a zero-value invoice |

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. A Purchase Manager who submits a usage log entry with a valid vendor, agreement item, future-dated date, and quantity receives a validation error: "Usage date cannot be in the future."
2. A usage log entry with quantity zero or negative is rejected with a validation error.
3. When Finance generates an invoice for a Per Unit item with three usage log entries (50, 80, 60 units), the invoice shows total quantity of 190.
4. When Finance generates an invoice for a Per Unit item with no usage logs, the invoice shows quantity of 1 and the corresponding amount.
5. Deleting a usage log entry reduces the cumulative total used in future invoice calculations.

### REQ-VND-006: Invoice Generation (Single and Batch)
**Priority:** Core (P0) | **Tags:** [WORKFLOW][CALCULATION]

**Description:**
Finance generates invoices for agreement items either one at a time or in bulk. The system automatically computes the invoice amount using the billing model, usage totals, tax percentages, and any adjustments (additional charges, discounts). Generated invoices are immutable financial records — subsequent changes to the agreement do not alter already-generated invoices.

**Actors:** Initiates: Finance Manager, Accountant | Processes: System (calculation engine, duplicate check, invoice numbering) | Views: Finance Manager, Accountant

**Business Rules:**

| BR Ref | Rule |
|--------|------|
| BR-VND-002 | The system must prevent generating a second invoice for the same agreement item and the same billing period (start date to end date) |
| BR-VND-003 | Invoices can only be generated for agreement items belonging to agreements in Active status |
| BR-VND-005 | Tax percentages, unit rates, and fixed charges are copied from the agreement item into the invoice at generation time; future changes to the agreement item do not affect this invoice |
| BR-VND-010 | Each invoice must have a unique number per vendor; the system generates this number automatically using a sequential scheme |
| BR-VND-008 | Billing cycle frequency is enforced: Monthly items allow one invoice per month; One-Time items allow one invoice per agreement lifetime |

**Invoice Amount Formula:**
1. Determine quantity used (from usage logs; default 1 if none)
2. Calculate sub-total per billing model (Fixed / Per Unit / Hybrid)
3. Sum all four tax percentages; apply to sub-total to get tax total
4. Net payable = sub-total + tax total + additional charges - discount amount
5. Due date = invoice date + payment terms days from agreement

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Finance who generates a Fixed-billing invoice for a Monthly agreement item in May sees a net payable of fixed-charge × (1 + combined tax rate) and a due date set according to the agreement's payment terms.
2. Generating an invoice for the same agreement item and same billing period a second time is blocked with a duplicate-prevention message.
3. Generating an invoice for a Draft or Expired agreement item is blocked with an appropriate status error.
4. Batch invoice generation for five items produces five invoices; if two items fail (e.g., duplicate), the three successful items are created and the two failures are reported individually — the successes are not rolled back.
5. A generated invoice retains the tax percentages from the agreement item at the time of generation, even if those percentages are later edited on the agreement item.
6. Finance without the "Generate Invoice" permission who clicks the Generate button receives an access-denied response.

### REQ-VND-007: Invoice PDF, ZIP Download, and Email Dispatch
**Priority:** Core (P0) | **Tags:** [WORKFLOW][NOTIFICATION]

**Description:**
Finance can download a formatted PDF of any invoice, download a ZIP archive of multiple invoice PDFs in one action, and dispatch invoices by email to vendors in the background. These actions support month-end billing operations without requiring the Finance Manager to handle each vendor individually.

**Actors:** Initiates: Finance Manager, Accountant | Processes: System (PDF rendering, ZIP creation, background email job)

**Business Rules:**
- Email dispatch is queued as a background job with three automatic retry attempts
- ZIP archive of PDFs is generated, downloaded, and then automatically deleted from temporary storage to prevent file accumulation
- The email sender is the logged-in Finance Manager's registered email address

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Finance who clicks "Download PDF" on a single invoice receives a formatted PDF containing vendor details, invoice number, billing period, line item breakdown, tax breakdown, and net payable.
2. Finance who selects three invoices and clicks "Download ZIP" receives a single ZIP file containing three individual PDFs.
3. After the ZIP download, no residual individual PDF files remain in server temporary storage.
4. Finance who clicks "Email to Vendor" for multiple invoices sees a success confirmation and the emails are delivered to each vendor's registered email address within the retry window.
5. A Finance Manager without the "Download Invoice" permission cannot access the PDF or ZIP download endpoints.

### REQ-VND-008: Payment Recording
**Priority:** Core (P0) | **Tags:** [DATA_ENTRY][WORKFLOW]

**Description:**
Finance records payments made to vendors against specific invoices. Multiple payments can be made against a single invoice (partial payments). On each payment, the invoice's paid amount is recalculated and the invoice status is updated automatically.

**Actors:** Initiates: Finance Manager, Accountant | Processes: System (balance recalculation, status update) | Views: Finance Manager, Accountant

**Business Rules:**

| BR Ref | Rule |
|--------|------|
| BR-VND-004 | The payment amount recorded must not exceed the remaining balance due on the invoice |
| BR-VND-009 | For electronic payment modes (NEFT, RTGS, UPI), the reference number is mandatory; for Cheque payments, the cheque number is mandatory |
| BR-VND-016 | If a payment record is deleted, the invoice's paid amount is recalculated and the invoice status is updated accordingly |

**Invoice Status After Payment:**
- No payment: Pending
- Partial payment (amount paid is greater than zero but less than net payable): Partially Paid
- Full payment (amount paid equals or exceeds net payable): Fully Paid

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Finance who records a payment of ₹10,000 against an invoice with net payable ₹25,000 sees the invoice status change to Partially Paid and balance due become ₹15,000.
2. Finance who records the remaining ₹15,000 against the same invoice sees the invoice status change to Fully Paid and balance due become ₹0.
3. Finance who attempts to record a payment of ₹30,000 against an invoice with balance due ₹15,000 receives a validation error: "Payment amount cannot exceed the remaining balance due."
4. Finance who selects NEFT as payment mode and leaves reference number blank receives a validation error.
5. Finance who deletes a payment of ₹10,000 against a Partially Paid invoice sees the invoice revert to Pending and balance due return to the full net payable.
6. The payment record stores the name of the logged-in user who recorded the payment.

### REQ-VND-009: Payment Reconciliation
**Priority:** Core (P0) | **Tags:** [WORKFLOW]

**Description:**
Finance can mark individual payment records as reconciled after verifying them against the school's bank statement. The system records who performed the reconciliation and when. Unreconciled payments are distinguishable in the payment list for follow-up.

**Actors:** Initiates: Finance Manager, Accountant | Views: Finance Manager, Accountant

**Business Rules:**
- Reconciliation records the name of the logged-in user and the exact timestamp
- A reconciled payment can be un-reconciled if marked in error (edit the payment record)

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Finance who marks a payment as reconciled sees the reconciled flag, the reconciling user's name, and the reconciliation timestamp displayed on the payment record.
2. Finance who filters the payment list by "Unreconciled" sees only payment records where the reconciled flag is No.
3. Finance who filters by payment status "INITIATED" sees only payments in that state.
4. Finance who edits a payment to un-reconcile it sees the reconciliation details cleared.

### REQ-VND-010: Vendor Dashboard
**Priority:** Standard (P1) | **Tags:** [DASHBOARD]

**Description:**
The dashboard is the first screen users see when opening the Vendor module. It presents real-time financial KPIs, expiry alerts, and activity summaries in a single view so Finance Managers and Principals can assess the school's vendor payment health without navigating through individual tabs.

**Actors:** Views: School Admin, Finance Manager, Accountant, Purchase Manager

**Key Metrics Displayed:**
- Total active vendors
- Total active agreements
- Agreements expiring within the next 30 days
- Total invoiced amount for the current calendar month
- Total amount paid in the current calendar month
- Total outstanding balance (all Pending and Partially Paid invoices)
- Payment completion rate (paid ÷ invoiced × 100)

**Sections:**
- Summary metric cards (with appropriate colour coding for urgency)
- Expiring agreements alert list (vendor name, reference, end date)
- Recent invoices (last 10: vendor, invoice number, date, net payable, status)
- Recent payments (last 10: vendor, date, amount, mode, reconciled flag)
- Top outstanding vendors (bar/list: top 5 vendors by outstanding balance)

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. A Finance Manager who opens the Vendor module sees the dashboard as the default first tab with all metric cards populated.
2. The "Expiring Soon" count reflects only Active agreements whose end date is within 30 days of today.
3. A user without "View Invoice" permission does not see invoice-related metric cards.
4. Dashboard figures change to reflect a payment made 1 minute ago when the page is refreshed.

### REQ-VND-012: Agreement Auto-Expiry (Scheduled)
**Priority:** Standard (P1) | **Tags:** [SCHEDULED][WORKFLOW][NOTIFICATION]

**Description:**
A scheduled daily process checks all Active agreements and automatically transitions any whose end date has passed to Expired status. The Finance Manager receives a notification listing agreements that expired in that run.

**Actors:** Initiates: System (daily schedule) | Notified: Finance Manager

**Business Rules:**

| BR Ref | Rule |
|--------|------|
| BR-VND-007 | Active agreements with end date before today's date are set to Expired by the daily process |

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. An Active agreement with end date of yesterday is shown as Expired the next morning after the scheduled process runs.
2. An Active agreement with end date of tomorrow is unchanged after the scheduled process runs.
3. The Finance Manager receives a notification listing each agreement that was expired in the run, with the vendor name and agreement reference.
4. Invoices already generated under an agreement that is now Expired remain unchanged — they are not cancelled by the expiry.
5. The process run is recorded in the system audit log.

|--------|------|

## Section 5 — Data Requirements

### 5.1 Business Entities

**Vendor** — the core master record representing a supplier. Each vendor has identity, contact, tax, and banking attributes. A vendor may have many agreements. All vendor data is scoped to one school.

**Vendor Item** — a catalogue entry for a type of good or service. Items are school-level (not vendor-specific at catalogue level) and reused across multiple agreements. An item has a type (service/product), category, unit, and HSN/SAC code.

**Vendor Agreement** — a formal contract between the school and one vendor. An agreement has a reference number, period, billing cycle, payment terms, and a status lifecycle (Draft → Active → Expired/Terminated). An agreement contains one or more agreement items.

**Agreement Item** — a line item within an agreement that defines what is billed, at what rate, and under which billing model. Each item may optionally link to a specific school asset (vehicle, personnel, building asset).

**Usage Log** — a consumption record: one vendor, one agreement item, one date, one quantity. Logs accumulate and are aggregated at invoice generation time for Per Unit and Hybrid items.

**Invoice** — a generated payable document. All billing parameters are snapshotted from the agreement item at generation time. An invoice carries computed amounts (sub-total, tax total, net payable), a running paid total, a computed balance due, and a status. An invoice may have many payment records.

**Payment** — a single payment transaction against one invoice. Captures date, amount, mode, reference, status, reconciliation flag, and the user who recorded it. Multiple payments may exist per invoice.

### 5.2 Privacy Classification

| Business Field | Entity | Classification | Reason |
|---------------|--------|---------------|--------|
| Vendor Name | Vendor | Internal | School-internal business information |
| Contact Number, Email | Vendor | Internal | Business contact details |
| GSTIN | Vendor | Confidential | Tax identifier — appears on invoices but sensitive |
| PAN Number | Vendor | Sensitive (PII) | Income tax identifier for the vendor entity |
| Bank Account Number | Vendor | Sensitive (PII) | Financial account detail — must be protected |
| IFSC Code | Vendor | Internal | Semi-public bank identifier |
| UPI ID | Vendor | Confidential | Payment identifier |
| Payment Amount | Invoice / Payment | Confidential | Financial transaction data |
| Reference Number | Payment | Internal | Bank transaction reference |

### 5.3 Relationships

- One Vendor has many Agreements; one Agreement belongs to one Vendor
- One Vendor has many Vendor Items in its agreements (via agreement items)
- One Agreement has one or more Agreement Items
- One Agreement Item may optionally link to one School Asset (vehicle, personnel, building asset)
- One Agreement Item has zero or many Usage Logs
- One Agreement Item has zero or many Invoices (typically one per billing period)
- One Invoice has zero or many Payment records

## Section 6 — Workflows

### Workflow 1: Vendor Onboarding
**Trigger:** New supplier begins providing goods or services to the school
**End States:** Vendor registered and active; or form abandoned

**Steps:**
1. [Finance Manager] Opens "Add Vendor" form
2. [Finance Manager] Enters vendor name, type (from dropdown), contact person, phone, email, address
3. [Finance Manager] Enters GSTIN (if registered) and PAN number
4. [Finance Manager] Enters bank name, account number, IFSC, branch name, UPI ID
5. [System] Validates GSTIN format (15 characters, standard pattern), PAN format (10 characters)
6. [System] Checks GSTIN and PAN uniqueness within the school
7. [Finance Manager] Optionally uploads vendor documents (GST certificate, registration certificate)
8. [System] Stores documents via media library; sets "document uploaded" flag to Yes
9. [System] Saves vendor record with Active status; logs the action
10. [Finance Manager] Vendor appears in vendor list and in all downstream agreement dropdowns

**Exception Paths:**
- GSTIN format invalid → Form returned with field-level error; vendor not saved
- GSTIN already registered for another vendor → Uniqueness error; vendor not saved
- Document upload fails → Vendor is saved; document upload re-attempted separately

**Notifications Triggered:** None (internal registration)

---

### Workflow 2: Agreement and Invoice Lifecycle
**Trigger:** School signs a contract with a vendor
**End States:** Invoices generated, payments recorded, agreement expired or terminated

**Steps:**
1. [Finance Manager] Creates agreement: vendor, reference number, start date, end date, billing cycle, payment terms; status = Draft
2. [Finance Manager] Uploads signed agreement PDF; "agreement uploaded" flag set to Yes
3. [Finance Manager] Adds one or more agreement items: select catalogue item, choose billing model, enter charges, set tax percentages, optionally link to asset
4. [Finance Manager] Activates agreement: status Draft → Active; system validates at least one active item exists
5. [For Per Unit / Hybrid items] [Finance Manager / Purchase Manager] Logs usage entries as services are consumed
6. [Finance Manager] Triggers invoice generation for one or more agreement items:
   a. System checks agreement is Active
   b. System checks no duplicate for same item + billing period
   c. System aggregates usage quantity (or defaults to 1)
   d. System calculates amount per billing model; snapshots tax and rate parameters
   e. System generates sequential invoice number; sets due date = invoice date + payment terms days
   f. Invoice created with status = Pending
7. [Finance Manager] Downloads PDF; optionally emails invoice to vendor
8. [Finance Manager] Records payment(s) as vendor is paid; invoice status progresses Pending → Partially Paid → Fully Paid
9. [Finance Manager] Reconciles payments against bank statement
10. [System — daily] Auto-expiry process: Active agreement whose end date < today → Expired; Finance Manager notified

**Exception Paths:**
- Invoice generation for Expired/Terminated agreement → Blocked with status error
- Duplicate invoice for same period → Blocked with duplicate-prevention message
- Batch generation — some items valid, some not → Partial success; failed items listed; successful items not rolled back
- Payment amount exceeds balance due → Blocked with validation error

**Notifications Triggered:**
- Step 10: Finance Manager notified when agreement auto-expires (list of expired agreements)

---

### Workflow 3: Batch Invoice Generation and ZIP PDF Download
**Trigger:** Month-end billing run
**End States:** Multiple invoices created; ZIP PDF downloaded

**Steps:**
1. [Finance Manager] Opens "Inv. Need To Generate" view; filters by vendor and date range
2. [Finance Manager] Selects multiple agreement items via checkboxes
3. [Finance Manager] Clicks "Generate Multiple Invoices"; optionally enters additional charges or discount
4. [System] Processes each item independently — each either succeeds or fails
5. [System] Reports summary: success count and list; failure count and per-item reasons
6. [Finance Manager] Selects generated invoices and clicks "Download PDF (Multiple)"
7. [System] Generates one DomPDF per invoice; creates ZIP archive in temporary storage
8. [System] Returns ZIP as browser download; deletes individual PDFs and ZIP from temporary storage immediately after dispatch

**Exception Paths:**
- One item fails (duplicate, wrong status) → That item is skipped; others proceed
- ZIP creation fails → Error returned; no partial archive delivered

---

### Workflow 4: Payment Reconciliation
**Trigger:** Bank statement received; Finance team matches payments
**End States:** All payments for the period are either reconciled or flagged for follow-up

**Steps:**
1. [Finance Manager] Opens Payment Details tab; filters by vendor and date range
2. [Finance Manager] For each payment entry, compares amount and reference to bank statement
3. [Finance Manager] Clicks Edit on a matched payment; checks the "Reconciled" toggle
4. [System] Records reconciliation timestamp and logged-in user's name
5. [Finance Manager] Unreconciled payments remain in filter view for follow-up

**Exception Paths:**
- Payment entry found in system but not on bank statement → Mark as FAILED; flag for re-initiation
- Bank statement amount differs from recorded amount → Edit payment record; re-reconcile

---

### Workflow 5: Agreement Auto-Expiry (Scheduled Daily)
**Trigger:** Daily scheduled run at 00:05
**End States:** All overdue agreements moved to Expired; Finance Manager notified

**Steps:**
1. [System] Queries all Active agreements where end date is before today
2. [System] For each: updates status to Expired; writes audit log entry
3. [System] Compiles list of expired agreements (vendor name, reference, original end date)
4. [System] Dispatches notification to Finance Manager with the compiled list
5. [Finance Manager] Reviews notification; initiates renewals as needed

**Exception Paths:**
- No agreements expired that day → No notification sent (or empty notification, implementation choice)

## Section 7 — Reporting and Analytics

### RPT-VND-001: Vendor Ledger Summary Report
**Audience:** Finance Manager, Principal | **Frequency:** On-demand | **Default Date Range:** Current month

**Purpose:** High-level snapshot of every vendor's complete transactional profile — total invoiced, total paid, total outstanding, overdue amount, and payment rate — within the selected date range.

**Summary Metrics:** Total registered vendors; active vendors; total invoiced amount; total amount paid; total outstanding; total overdue (past due date with balance remaining); payment collection rate (total paid ÷ total invoiced × 100)

**Visuals:** Spend breakdown by vendor type (pie chart); top 5 outstanding vendors (bar chart); monthly billing trend — invoiced vs paid (line/bar chart)

**Data Grid Columns:** Vendor Name | Vendor Type | Contact Person | Contact Number | Total Agreements | Active Agreements | Total Invoices | Total Invoiced Amount | Total Paid Amount | Total Outstanding | Overdue Amount | Last Invoice Date | Last Payment Date | Status

**Export:** PDF, Excel, CSV

**Rules:**
- Only active (non-deleted) invoices count toward invoiced metrics
- Only SUCCESS-status, non-deleted payment records count toward paid metrics
- Overdue days = current date minus due date, applied only when due date is past and balance due is greater than zero
