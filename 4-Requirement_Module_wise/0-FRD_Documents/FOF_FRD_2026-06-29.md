## Section 1 — Module Overview

### 1.1 Business Purpose

The school front office is the first point of contact for every visitor, telephone caller, walk-in enquiry, piece of incoming or outgoing mail, and daily administrative request. In most Indian K-12 schools these activities live in paper registers — a visitor book, a phone diary, a dak (mail) register, a dispatch register, a key register, a lost-and-found log — which cannot be searched, audited, or retrieved during a CBSE/State Board inspection. The FrontOffice module replaces all of these with a single digital system that logs every front-desk activity in real time, automates approvals (gate passes, circulars, certificates), and gives the principal a live operational snapshot of the reception desk. Without it, a school has no reliable record of who entered the campus, which student left mid-day and with whom, whether a circular actually reached parents, or how many certificate requests are pending.

### 1.2 Business Value

- **Inspection-ready records:** Every visitor, certificate, circular, and government-notice receipt is permanently logged and retrievable for CBSE/State Board audits — replacing paper registers that fail inspections.
- **Student safety & accountability:** Gate passes and early departures capture who collected which student, with identity proof — a verifiable safety trail for mid-day pickups.
- **Communication that is provably delivered:** Circulars move through a draft → approve → distribute lifecycle with a per-recipient delivery log, ending "I never received it" disputes.
- **Reduced front-desk workload:** Auto-generated pass/request numbers, templated communication, and self-service certificate/appointment requests cut manual effort.
- **Real-time visibility:** A live dashboard shows visitors on campus, pending approvals, overdue keys, and unresolved complaints at a glance.
- **Cross-module automation:** Early departures update Attendance automatically; certificate issuance checks fee clearance; front-office complaints escalate into the full Complaint module.

### 1.3 Scope

#### In Scope

1. Visitor registration, visitor pass printing, checkout, and overstay flagging
2. Visitor purpose master configuration (including the government-inspection retention flag)
3. Student and staff gate-pass issuance with an approval workflow
4. Student early-departure logging with automatic Attendance synchronisation
5. Phone call diary (incoming and outgoing) with follow-up flags
6. Postal/courier register (inward and outward) with acknowledgement locking
7. Dispatch register for official outgoing correspondence
8. Circular management — draft, approval, audience-targeted distribution, and archive
9. Digital notice board with display windows, pinning, and emergency override
10. Bulk email and SMS communication with templates and per-recipient delivery logging
11. School calendar event management (public-facing events)
12. Appointment scheduling with staff confirmation and slot-conflict checking
13. Lost-and-found register with claim matching and disposal tracking
14. Key management register (issue/return with overdue flagging)
15. Emergency contact directory
16. Certificate request, multi-stage approval, and PDF issuance (Bonafide, Character, TC, Migration, etc.)
17. Lightweight front-office complaint intake with escalation to the Complaint module
18. Feedback form creation, anonymous public submission, and response reporting
19. A front-office operational dashboard

#### Out of Scope

1. **Gate security, biometrics, and vehicle logging** — handled by the VSM (Visitor Security) module; FOF receives a pre-registered-visitor handoff from VSM
2. **Full grievance workflow with SLA and escalation tiers** — owned by the Complaint (CMP) module; FOF only does lightweight intake and escalates
3. **Attendance marking logic** — owned by the Attendance (ATT) module; FOF only triggers the absence sync for early departures
4. **Fee balances and clearance calculation** — owned by the StudentFee module; FOF only consumes a clearance check before issuing TC/Migration certificates
5. **The actual email/SMS gateway delivery** — owned by the Notification (NTF) module; FOF composes and logs, NTF delivers
6. **Parent/Student portal screens** — the Parent Portal (PPT) and Student Portal (STP) modules own their own UIs; they read FOF circulars, notices, events, and submit certificate requests

### 1.4 Key Terminology

| Business Term | Meaning |
|---------------|---------|
| Visitor Register | The official digital log of every person entering the campus; required for security and board inspection |
| Visitor Pass | A printed slip issued to a registered visitor (format VP-YYYYMMDD-NNN) |
| Overstay | A visitor who entered but was not checked out by school closing time; flagged automatically |
| Gate Pass | An authorisation slip for a student or staff member to leave campus during school hours and return (format GP-YYYYMMDD-NNN) |
| Early Departure | A student collected by a parent/guardian before the school day ends and who does **not** return; updates Attendance (format ED-YYYYMMDD-NNN) |
| Dak / Postal Register | The log of physical mail and couriers received (inward) or sent (outward), with tracking numbers (formats IN-YYYY-NNNN / OUT-YYYY-NNNN) |
| Dispatch Register | The log of official outgoing letters, documents, and cheques (format DSP-YYYY-NNNN) |
| Circular | A formal, numbered, dated school communication issued to parents and/or staff (format CIR-YYYY-NNNN) — distinct from an informal notice |
| Notice | A digital notice-board announcement with a display window and target audience |
| Appointment | A booked meeting with the principal, vice-principal, or a staff member (format APT-YYYYMMDD-NNN) |
| Certificate | A formal document issued to a student (Bonafide, Character, TC, Migration, etc.); request format CERT-YYYY-NNNNN, issued number per type (e.g., BON-YYYY-NNN) |
| TC / Migration Certificate | Transfer Certificate / board-migration certificate; issuance requires fee clearance |
| Emergency Contact Directory | The school's curated list of external emergency numbers (hospital, police, fire, ambulance, transport) |
| Government Inspection Visit | A visitor purpose flagged so the visitor record can never be deleted (permanent retention) |
| ATT Sync | The automatic update sent to the Attendance module when a student departs early, marking them absent for the remaining periods |

### 3.1 Visitor Purpose Configuration

**Requirement ID:** REQ-FOF-001
**Priority:** Standard (P1)
**Category Tags:** [CONFIGURATION]

#### Business Description
School Admin maintains the master list of visitor purposes (e.g., Parent Meeting, Government Inspection, Job Interview, Delivery/Courier, Sales Visit, Alumni Visit, Emergency) that the receptionist selects from when registering a visitor. Each purpose can be flagged as a government-inspection visit, which causes any visitor recorded under it to be permanently retained and protected from deletion. Purposes can be activated, deactivated, sorted, and soft-deleted.

#### Actors
- **Initiates:** School Admin
- **Processes / Approves:** School Admin
- **Views / Receives notification:** Receptionist (purposes appear in the registration dropdown)

#### Business Rules
| Rule ID | Business Rule | Rule Type |
|---------|--------------|-----------|
| BR-FOF-007 | A visitor purpose flagged as a government-inspection visit causes every visitor registered under it to be permanently retained and protected from deletion. | Permission |

#### Acceptance Criteria
1. School Admin can create, edit, reorder, activate/deactivate, and soft-delete a visitor purpose
2. A purpose flagged as government-inspection is clearly marked in the list
3. A deactivated purpose no longer appears in the visitor-registration dropdown
4. The standard purpose set is pre-seeded for a new school

#### Integration with Other Modules
- Sends to: Visitor Registration (REQ-FOF-003) — supplies the purpose dropdown and the retention flag

### 3.3 Visitor Registration & Pass

**Requirement ID:** REQ-FOF-003
**Priority:** Core (P0)
**Category Tags:** [DATA_ENTRY] [WORKFLOW]

#### Business Description
The receptionist registers every person entering the campus, capturing their name, mobile, ID proof type and number, organisation, purpose of visit, the person/department they are meeting, optional vehicle number, accompanying-persons count, and an optional photograph. On registration the system records the entry time, sets status to "In", and generates a unique visitor pass number. A printable A6 visitor pass (school name/logo, purpose, entry time, valid-until end of day) is produced without requiring a PDF download. When the visitor leaves, the receptionist checks them out, recording the exit time. Visitors not checked out by school closing time are automatically flagged as "Overstay". If the visitor was pre-registered at the gate by VSM, their details are auto-populated from the handoff.

#### Actors
- **Initiates:** Receptionist, School Admin
- **Processes / Approves:** System (pass number, entry time, overstay flagging)
- **Views / Receives notification:** Person being met (optional), Principal (government-visit visibility)

#### Business Rules
| Rule ID | Business Rule | Rule Type |
|---------|--------------|-----------|
| BR-FOF-001 | A visitor's ID proof type and ID proof number must be captured at registration. | Validation |
| BR-FOF-002 | A visitor who has not been checked out by the school's configured closing time is automatically flagged "Overstay". | Workflow |
| BR-FOF-007 | A visitor registered under a government-inspection purpose cannot be deleted (permanent retention). | Permission |
| BR-FOF-015 | Aadhar ID proof numbers are displayed showing only the last four digits; the full number is stored under the tenant's encryption policy. | Permission |
| BR-FOF-016 | Every visitor pass number is system-generated, unique within the school, and follows the format VP-YYYYMMDD-NNN. | Validation |

#### Acceptance Criteria
1. Registering a visitor captures name, mobile, ID proof type + number, and purpose (all required) and generates a pass number in the format VP-YYYYMMDD-NNN
2. A printable visitor pass is available immediately, optimised for print without a PDF download
3. Checkout records the exit time and sets status to "Out"
4. After closing time, a visitor still showing "In" is automatically set to "Overstay"
5. A visitor under a government-inspection purpose cannot be deleted — the action is blocked with a clear message
6. An Aadhar number shows only its last four digits on screen
7. A VSM pre-registered visitor's details are auto-populated when the gate handoff is present

#### Integration with Other Modules
- Receives from: VSM — pre-registered visitor handoff; GlobalMaster — ID proof types, country/state; System — staff lookup and photo storage
- Sends to: Dashboard (REQ-FOF-019) — live visitor counts

#### Enhancement Notes (Future)
- Visitor self-registration kiosk (ENH-FOF-001); webcam photo capture (ENH-FOF-002); staff pre-registration of expected visitors (ENH-FOF-011)

### 3.4 Gate Pass Management

**Requirement ID:** REQ-FOF-004
**Priority:** Core (P0)
**Category Tags:** [WORKFLOW] [APPROVAL] [NOTIFICATION]

#### Business Description
When a student or staff member needs to leave campus during school hours and return, a gate pass is raised capturing the person, exit purpose (Medical, Personal, Official, Sports, Family Emergency, Other), purpose details, and expected return time. The pass enters a Pending Approval state and notifies the approving authority (Principal/Vice Principal/HOD). For students, a parent notification is dispatched automatically. Once approved, the front desk marks the person "Exited" when they leave and "Returned" when they re-enter. A student may hold only one active gate pass at a time. Approved passes are printable.

#### Actors
- **Initiates:** Receptionist, Class Teacher (for own students)
- **Processes / Approves:** Principal, Vice Principal, HOD
- **Views / Receives notification:** Approving authority (pending alert), Parent (student passes), Front desk (decision)

#### Business Rules
| Rule ID | Business Rule | Rule Type |
|---------|--------------|-----------|
| BR-FOF-003 | A student gate pass must dispatch a parent notification; failure to notify is surfaced to the front desk. | Workflow |
| BR-FOF-004 | A student may hold only one active gate pass (Pending Approval, Approved, or Exited) at a time; a second request is blocked until the current pass is Returned or Cancelled. | Validation |
| BR-FOF-016 | Every gate pass number is system-generated, unique, and follows the format GP-YYYYMMDD-NNN. | Validation |
| BR-FOF-022 | A gate pass may only follow valid transitions: Pending Approval → Approved or Rejected or Cancelled; Approved → Exited → Returned. Any other transition is blocked. | Workflow |

#### Acceptance Criteria
1. A gate pass is created with person type, person, purpose, and expected return time, in status Pending Approval, and the approver is notified
2. For a student pass, a parent notification is dispatched automatically and the dispatch outcome is recorded
3. A second active gate pass for the same student is rejected with a clear message
4. Approval records the approver and timestamp; an approved pass is printable
5. The front desk can mark Exited and then Returned, and no invalid status jump is permitted

#### Integration with Other Modules
- Receives from: StudentProfile (students), System (staff)
- Sends to: Notification — approval and parent-notification messages

#### Enhancement Notes (Future)
- WhatsApp channel for approvals and parent alerts (ENH-FOF-003)

### 3.5 Student Early Departure

**Requirement ID:** REQ-FOF-005
**Priority:** Core (P0)
**Category Tags:** [WORKFLOW] [INTEGRATION] [NOTIFICATION]

#### Business Description
When a parent or guardian collects a student before the school day ends (and the student does not return), the receptionist logs an early departure: the student, departure time, reason, and the collecting person's name, relationship, and ID proof. The system records the event, triggers the Attendance module to mark the student absent for the remaining periods, and dispatches a confirmation notification to the parent. The Attendance synchronisation status is tracked (Pending / Synced / Failed); a failed sync is surfaced prominently to the receptionist and retried — it must never fail silently. This is distinct from a gate pass because the student does not return.

#### Actors
- **Initiates:** Receptionist
- **Processes / Approves:** System (Attendance sync, notification)
- **Views / Receives notification:** Parent (confirmation), Receptionist (sync-failure alert)

#### Business Rules
| Rule ID | Business Rule | Rule Type |
|---------|--------------|-----------|
| BR-FOF-013 | An early departure must synchronise to the Attendance module to mark the student absent for the remaining periods; a failed sync is surfaced to the receptionist and queued for retry (never silent). | Workflow |
| BR-FOF-016 | Every early-departure number is system-generated, unique, and follows the format ED-YYYYMMDD-NNN. | Validation |

#### Acceptance Criteria
1. An early departure is logged with student, time, reason, and the collecting person's name, relationship, and ID proof
2. The Attendance module is called to mark the student absent from the departure period onward
3. The parent receives a confirmation notification
4. If the Attendance sync fails, the departure record is kept, the sync status shows "Failed", and a front-desk alert is raised
5. Today's early departures are listed live with their sync-status badges

#### Integration with Other Modules
- Sends to: Attendance — absence for remaining periods; Notification — parent confirmation
- Receives from: StudentProfile — student lookup

#### Enhancement Notes (Future)
- Log departure from a mobile device (API already specified)

### 3.9 Circular Management

**Requirement ID:** REQ-FOF-009
**Priority:** Core (P0)
**Category Tags:** [WORKFLOW] [APPROVAL] [NOTIFICATION]

#### Business Description
A circular is a formal school communication issued to parents and/or staff. A Communication Manager or Admin drafts it with a title, subject, rich-text body, audience (Parents / Staff / Both / Specific Class / Specific Section), effective date, and optional attachment. It moves through Draft → Pending Approval → Approved → Distributed. On distribution, the system resolves the recipient list from the audience configuration, dispatches via the Notification module (email and optional SMS), and creates a per-recipient distribution record tracking Queued/Sent/Delivered/Failed. An approved circular cannot be edited — a new version must be created. Rejected circulars return to Draft with notes. All distributed circulars are available in a searchable archive and in the Parent Portal.

#### Actors
- **Initiates:** Communication Manager, School Admin
- **Processes / Approves:** Principal, School Admin
- **Views / Receives notification:** Approver (pending alert), Parents/Staff (distribution)

#### Business Rules
| Rule ID | Business Rule | Rule Type |
|---------|--------------|-----------|
| BR-FOF-008 | An Approved or Distributed circular cannot be edited; a new version must be created. A rejected circular returns to Draft with rejection notes. | Workflow |
| BR-FOF-016 | Circular numbers are system-generated and unique, following the format CIR-YYYY-NNNN. | Validation |
| BR-FOF-018 | Distribution must resolve recipients strictly from the audience configuration — e.g., a "Specific Class" circular reaches only that class's parents. | Workflow |
| BR-FOF-023 | A circular may only follow valid transitions: Draft → Pending Approval → Approved → Distributed (or Recalled); a rejection returns it to Draft. | Workflow |

#### Acceptance Criteria
1. A circular can be drafted, submitted, approved, and distributed, with the approver notified at the pending stage
2. Editing an Approved or Distributed circular is blocked; a new version is required
3. Distribution reaches exactly the audience configured (verified for a Specific-Class circular)
4. A per-recipient distribution log shows Sent/Delivered/Failed counts
5. Distributed circulars appear in the searchable archive

#### Integration with Other Modules
- Receives from: SchoolSetup — classes/sections for targeting; System — recipient users
- Sends to: Notification — per-recipient email/SMS; Parent Portal — circular archive

#### Enhancement Notes (Future)
- Searchable digital circular archive for parents (ENH-FOF-004)

### 3.11 Email & SMS Communication

**Requirement ID:** REQ-FOF-011
**Priority:** Standard (P1)
**Category Tags:** [NOTIFICATION] [INTEGRATION]

#### Business Description
The Communication Manager sends bulk email and SMS to students, staff, or parents — either all of a group or filtered by class/section. Email supports reusable templates with placeholders. SMS shows a live character counter and a multi-SMS cost warning before sending. Actual delivery is performed by the Notification module; FOF provides the composition UI, template management, and a front-office-scoped audit log with per-recipient delivery status fed from the gateway webhook.

#### Actors
- **Initiates:** Communication Manager, School Admin
- **Processes / Approves:** System (via Notification)
- **Views / Receives notification:** Recipients (students/staff/parents)

#### Business Rules
| Rule ID | Business Rule | Rule Type |

## Section 6 — Workflows

### 6.1 Visitor Lifecycle
**Trigger:** A visitor arrives at reception. **End State:** Out (or Overstay).
**Steps:** 1. Receptionist registers visitor → pass number + entry time set, status In. 2. Receptionist marks checkout → exit time set, status Out. **Decision:** if not checked out by closing time → System sets status Overstay.
**Exception paths:** Government-inspection visitor cannot be deleted; VSM handoff pre-populates the form.
**Notifications:** Optional alert to the person being met.

### 6.2 Gate Pass Approval & Exit
**Trigger:** A gate-pass request is raised. **End State:** Returned (or Rejected/Cancelled).
**Steps:** 1. Issue → status Pending Approval; approver notified; for students, parent notified. 2. Approver approves/rejects → status Approved/Rejected; front desk notified. 3. Front desk marks Exited on departure. 4. Front desk marks Returned on re-entry.
**Exception paths:** A second active pass for the same student is blocked; rejection records a reason.
**Notifications:** Approver (pending), Parent (student exit), Front desk (decision).

### 6.3 Student Early Departure & Attendance Sync
**Trigger:** A parent collects a student mid-day. **End State:** Departure logged and Attendance synced.
**Steps:** 1. Receptionist logs departure with collecting-person ID. 2. System dispatches a parent confirmation. 3. System calls Attendance to mark the student absent for remaining periods. **Decision:** sync success → status Synced; sync failure → status Failed, queued for retry, front-desk alert raised.
**Exception paths:** Attendance unavailable → retry queue; never silent.
**Notifications:** Parent (confirmation), Receptionist (sync failure).

### 6.4 Circular Draft → Approve → Distribute
**Trigger:** A circular is drafted. **End State:** Distributed.
**Steps:** 1. Draft created → status Draft. 2. Submitted → Pending Approval; approver notified. 3. Approver approves → Approved (or rejects → back to Draft with notes). 4. Distribution triggered → recipients resolved from audience config; Notification dispatches email/SMS; per-recipient distribution records created; status Distributed.
**Exception paths:** Approved circular cannot be edited (new version required); a recall sets status Recalled.
**Notifications:** Approver (pending), Parents/Staff (distribution).

### 6.5 Certificate Request → Approve → Issue
**Trigger:** A certificate is requested. **End State:** Issued (or Rejected/Cancelled).
**Steps:** 1. Request created → status Pending Approval. 2. Front office verifies; authority approves → Approved (or rejects → Rejected with reason, applicant notified). 3. For TC/Migration, fee clearance checked with StudentFee. 4. Issue → PDF generated, unique per-type certificate number assigned, receiver recorded, status Issued.
**Exception paths:** Outstanding fees block issuance of TC/Migration; issuance only from Approved state.
**Notifications:** Applicant (approval/rejection/issuance).

### 6.6 Appointment Booking → Confirmation
**Trigger:** An appointment is requested. **End State:** Completed (or No_Show/Cancelled).
**Steps:** 1. Booking created → status Pending; slot-conflict check; staff notified. 2. Staff confirms (or proposes alternate) → Confirmed; visitor notified. 3. On the day → Completed, or auto-flagged No_Show.
**Exception paths:** Overlapping slot for the same staff member is blocked; cancellation records a reason.
**Notifications:** Staff (request), Visitor/Parent (confirmation).

### 6.7 Front-Office Complaint → Resolve / Escalate
**Trigger:** A walk-in complaint is registered. **End State:** Resolved or Escalated.
**Steps:** 1. Complaint logged → assignee notified. 2. Assignee records resolution → Resolved. **Decision:** if escalation needed → a linked Complaint-module record is created and status set to Escalated.
**Exception paths:** Escalation hands off to the full CMP workflow.
**Notifications:** Assigned staff.

## Section 7 — Reporting & Analytics Requirements

### 7.1 Visitor Register Report
**Report ID:** RPT-FOF-001 | **Audience:** School Admin, Principal | **Frequency:** As-needed (inspections)
**Purpose:** An inspection-ready log of all visitors over a date range, including government-inspection visits.
**Contents:** Date, pass number, visitor name, purpose, person met, in/out times, status, government-visit flag.
**Filters:** Date range, purpose, status, government-visit only. **Export:** PDF, Excel.
**Report rules:** Government-inspection visits are always included and clearly marked.

### 7.2 Daily Front Office Activity Summary
**Report ID:** RPT-FOF-002 | **Audience:** School Admin, Receptionist | **Frequency:** Daily
**Purpose:** A single end-of-day summary across all front-desk registers.
**Contents:** Counts of visitors, calls, inward/outward mail, dispatches, gate passes, early departures, certificates issued.
**Filters:** Date. **Export:** PDF, Excel.

### 7.3 Gate Pass & Early Departure Report
**Report ID:** RPT-FOF-003 | **Audience:** School Admin, Principal | **Frequency:** Weekly
**Purpose:** Track student/staff exits and mid-day pickups, including Attendance-sync status.
**Contents:** Pass/departure number, person, purpose/reason, times, status, parent-notified/ATT-sync status.
**Filters:** Date range, person type, status. **Export:** PDF, Excel.

### 7.4 Certificate Issuance Register
**Report ID:** RPT-FOF-004 | **Audience:** School Admin | **Frequency:** As-needed
**Purpose:** A compliance register of all certificates issued.
**Contents:** Certificate number, type, student, request date, issue date, issued-by, receiver.
**Filters:** Date range, certificate type, student. **Export:** PDF, Excel.
**Report rules:** Only Issued certificates appear; numbers are unique per type per year.

### 7.5 Circular Distribution Report
**Report ID:** RPT-FOF-005 | **Audience:** School Admin, Communication Manager | **Frequency:** Per circular
**Purpose:** Prove delivery of a circular to its audience.
**Contents:** Circular number, audience, recipient counts by status (Queued/Sent/Delivered/Failed).
**Filters:** Circular, date range, channel. **Export:** PDF, Excel.

### 7.6 Postal & Dispatch Register Report
**Report ID:** RPT-FOF-006 | **Audience:** School Admin | **Frequency:** Monthly
**Purpose:** Combined inward/outward mail and official dispatch audit.
**Contents:** Reference number, type, date, sender/recipient/addressee, document type, acknowledgement/dispatch status.
**Filters:** Date range, type (inward/outward/dispatch), document type. **Export:** PDF, Excel.

### 7.7 Complaint & Feedback Summary
**Report ID:** RPT-FOF-007 | **Audience:** School Admin, Principal | **Frequency:** Monthly
**Purpose:** Front-office service-quality overview.
**Contents:** Complaints by type/urgency/status (incl. escalated count); feedback response counts and average ratings per form.
**Filters:** Date range, complaint type, form. **Export:** PDF, Excel.

## Section 9 — Non-Functional Requirements

### 9.1 Performance Expectations
| Requirement | Standard |
|-------------|---------|
| Visitor registration | Completes within 1 second |
| Visitor list | Loads within 2 seconds |
| Throughput | Supports 300+ visitor registrations per day per school |
| Overstay job | Closing-time flagging completes within 60 seconds for a full day's visitors |
| Circular distribution | Recipient resolution and dispatch for a full school (10,000+ recipients) handled asynchronously without blocking the UI |

### 9.2 Security Requirements (Business Language)
| Requirement | Rule |
|-------------|------|
| Access control | Each screen is restricted to roles holding the correct front-office permission |
| Data isolation | One school's front-office data is never visible to another school (per-tenant isolation) |
| ID privacy | Aadhar numbers display only the last four digits; full numbers are stored under the tenant encryption policy |
| Audit trail | Certificate issuances, circular distributions, and government-visit records are written to the activity log; deletions are soft-deletes |
| Permanent retention | Government-inspection visitor records can never be deleted |
| Anonymity | Anonymous feedback never stores the respondent's identity |

### 9.3 Usability Requirements
| Requirement | Standard |
|-------------|---------|
| Tablet support | Visitor registration and early-departure forms are usable on a tablet (the receptionist's common device) |
| Print support | Visitor pass, gate pass, and early-departure slips are print-optimised via print CSS — no PDF download required |
| Localisation | Certificate templates support regional-language content; interface strings are translatable |
| Resilience | If the Notification channel is unavailable, communication degrades gracefully with a retry queue rather than failing the user's action |
| Real-time availability | The visitor dashboard is available throughout school hours (≈7 AM–6 PM local) |
