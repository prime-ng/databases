## Section 1 — Module Overview

### 1.1 Business Purpose

The Inventory module gives an Indian K-12 school a single, controlled system to manage every item it buys, stores, issues and owns — from classroom stationery and laboratory chemicals to sports gear, furniture and IT equipment. It covers the full lifecycle: defining the item catalogue, requesting and ordering goods from suppliers, receiving and quality-checking deliveries, storing stock across multiple storerooms, issuing material to departments, tracking fixed assets and their upkeep, and raising alerts when stock runs low. Without this module, schools rely on spreadsheets and registers that lose track of what was bought, what is in stock, what each department consumed, and what the school owns — leading to wastage, duplicate purchases, missing assets and failed audit inspections.

### 1.2 Business Value

- Eliminates stock-outs and over-buying through automatic reorder alerts and reorder-quantity suggestions.
- Creates a clean audit trail for every purchase, receipt, issue and adjustment — essential for government-aided school inspections and annual stock audits.
- Gives the Principal and Accountant real-time visibility of stock value tied up in stores and the consumption pattern of every department.
- Enforces competitive purchasing (requisition → quotation → order → receipt) so spending is justified and traceable.
- Maintains a complete fixed-asset register with warranty, maintenance and disposal history, protecting the school's capital investment.
- Keeps stock records and the school's accounts automatically in step, so every goods receipt and issue is reflected in the books without re-keying.

### 1.3 Scope

#### In Scope

1. Item catalogue: item categories (stock groups), individual items, units of measure and conversion rules.
2. Storage locations (storerooms / godowns), including sub-locations and an in-charge person.
3. Stock movement ledger and live stock balance per item per location, with valuation.
4. Stock transfers between storage locations.
5. Physical stock counting, variance calculation and approved stock adjustments.
6. Supplier linkage to items and supplier rate contracts (agreed annual prices).
7. Procurement workflow: purchase requisition (indent) → quotation comparison → purchase order → goods receipt with quality check.
8. Departmental stock issue: issue request → approval → issue → acknowledgment, including direct issue.
9. Fixed-asset register: asset tagging, transfer, condition tracking, maintenance scheduling and disposal.
10. Reorder automation: low-stock alerts and optional automatic draft requisitions.
11. Barcode / QR label printing for items and asset tags.
12. Inventory reports, exports and a management dashboard.

#### Out of Scope

1. Library books and reading material — managed by the **Library** module.
2. Vehicle fuel, tyres and spare parts — managed by the **Transport** module.
3. Kitchen / canteen provisions and meal stock — managed by the **Cafeteria** module.
4. The supplier master record (supplier name, contacts, bank details) — owned by the **Vendor** module; Inventory only links to it.
5. Accounting vouchers, ledgers, tax masters and depreciation computation — owned by the **Accounting** module; Inventory triggers them but does not maintain them.
6. Supplier payments and settlement — owned by the **Payment** / **Accounting** modules.
7. Staff and department master data — owned by the **SchoolSetup** module.

### 1.4 Key Terminology

| Business Term | Meaning |
|---------------|---------|

## Section 3 — Functional Requirements

### 3.1 Stock Group (Category) Management
**Requirement ID:** REQ-INV-001
**Priority:** Core (P0)
**Category Tags:** [DATA_ENTRY] [CONFIGURATION]

#### Business Description
The school organises all items into a tree of categories called stock groups (for example, Stationery, Lab Equipment, Sports Equipment). A starter set of ten common groups is provided when the school is first set up, and the Store Manager can add, rename, re-order or deactivate groups, including multi-level sub-categories. Each group can carry a default unit of measure so new items in that group inherit it. Categories make the catalogue navigable and let reports be grouped sensibly.

#### Actors
- **Initiates:** Store Manager, School Admin
- **Processes / Approves:** Store Manager, School Admin
- **Views / Receives notification:** Principal, Accountant (view in reports)

#### Business Rules
| Rule ID | Business Rule | Rule Type |
|---------|---------------|-----------|
| BR-INV-001 | A stock group must have a name (up to 100 characters); if a short code is entered it must be unique. | Validation |
| BR-INV-002 | Pre-supplied (system) stock groups cannot be deleted, and any group that has items or child groups linked to it cannot be deleted — it may only be deactivated. | Workflow |

#### Acceptance Criteria
1. A user can create a stock group, set its parent, default unit and display order, and see it in the category tree.
2. A user cannot save a second stock group with a code that already exists.
3. Attempting to delete a system group or a group that has items/children is blocked with a clear message, and deactivation is offered instead.
4. Deactivating a group is recorded in the audit trail with the user and time.

#### Integration with Other Modules
- Receives from: None.
- Sends to: None (consumed internally by the item master and reports).

#### Enhancement Notes (Future)
- None.

### 3.2 Stock Item Master
**Requirement ID:** REQ-INV-002
**Priority:** Core (P0)
**Category Tags:** [DATA_ENTRY] [CONFIGURATION]

#### Business Description
The item master is the catalogue of every item the school can buy, store or issue. For each item the Store Manager records its name and optional stock-keeping code, its category and unit, whether it is a consumable or a fixed asset, its reorder and minimum/maximum stock levels, tax classification, brand/model, warranty (for assets), and whether batch and expiry must be tracked. The item also records its valuation method and an opening balance for first-time setup. A complete item master is the foundation for ordering, receiving, issuing and valuing stock.

#### Actors
- **Initiates:** Store Manager, School Admin
- **Processes / Approves:** Store Manager, School Admin
- **Views / Receives notification:** Principal, Accountant, Department Head (view)

#### Business Rules
| Rule ID | Business Rule | Rule Type |
|---------|---------------|-----------|
| BR-INV-003 | An item must have a name and must be assigned to a stock group and a default unit of measure; if a stock-keeping code is entered it must be unique. | Validation |
| BR-INV-004 | An item's opening balance (quantity, rate and value) can be entered or changed only before the item's first stock movement; afterwards it is locked. | Workflow |
| BR-INV-005 | An item marked as a fixed asset must carry an asset category and a warranty period so that an asset register record can be created when it is received. | Validation |
| BR-INV-006 | An item that has any stock balance or movement history cannot be deleted; it may only be deactivated. | Validation |

#### Acceptance Criteria
1. A user can create a consumable item and a fixed-asset item with all attributes, and the item appears in item lists and lookups.
2. The system rejects a duplicate stock-keeping code and an item saved without a category or unit.
3. Opening balance fields become read-only once the item has its first receipt or issue.
4. Deleting an item with stock or history is blocked, and deactivation is offered.

#### Integration with Other Modules
- Receives from: Accounting (tax classification and purchase/sales ledgers for the item).
- Sends to: None directly; the item is referenced by procurement, issue, asset and report features.

### 3.6 Stock Movement Ledger & Running Balances
**Requirement ID:** REQ-INV-006
**Priority:** Core (P0)
**Category Tags:** [DATA_ENTRY] [INTEGRATION]

#### Business Description
Every change to stock — a receipt, an issue, a transfer or an adjustment — is recorded as a permanent entry in the stock movement ledger, and the live balance (quantity and value) for each item in each storeroom is kept up to date instantly. The ledger is the single source of truth for what moved, when, by whom and why; the balance lets any screen or report show current stock without re-totalling history. Posted movements are never edited or deleted — corrections are made only through the adjustment process — and the system keeps the balance accurate even when several people record movements at the same time.

#### Actors
- **Initiates:** System (on receipt, issue, transfer or adjustment), Store Manager
- **Processes / Approves:** System
- **Views / Receives notification:** Store Manager, Principal, Accountant, Department Head (view)

#### Business Rules
| Rule ID | Business Rule | Rule Type |
|---------|---------------|-----------|
| BR-INV-011 | Every stock movement must be linked to an accounting voucher reference; no stock movement may exist without one. | Validation |
| BR-INV-012 | A posted stock movement can never be changed or deleted; any correction is made through a new stock adjustment. | Workflow |
| BR-INV-013 | The running balance for an item in a storeroom is updated within the same action that records the movement, so balance and ledger never disagree. | Calculation |
| BR-INV-014 | Negative stock is not permitted; an issue, transfer-out or outward adjustment that would take a balance below zero is rejected with a clear message. | Validation |
| BR-INV-015 | When two stock movements affect the same item and storeroom at the same time, the balance is protected so the two cannot overwrite each other. | Concurrency |
| BR-INV-056 | All inventory data belongs to a single school and is never visible to any other school. | Permission |
| BR-INV-057 | Every create, update, approval and stock movement is recorded with the user who did it and the time it happened. | Workflow |

#### Acceptance Criteria
1. A receipt, issue, transfer or adjustment each creates a ledger entry and immediately updates the item's balance in the affected storeroom.
2. The system refuses any attempt to edit or delete a posted ledger entry.
3. An issue that exceeds available stock is rejected and no balance change occurs.
4. Two simultaneous movements on the same item and storeroom both complete with a correct final balance.
5. A balance can be rebuilt from the ledger if a reconciliation is requested, and matches the recorded balance.

#### Integration with Other Modules
- Receives from: Accounting (the voucher reference linked to each movement).
- Sends to: Accounting (movements drive purchase and stock-journal vouchers — see Section 6).

#### Enhancement Notes (Future)
- None.

### 3.7 Stock Transfer Between Storerooms
**Requirement ID:** REQ-INV-007
**Priority:** Standard (P1)
**Category Tags:** [WORKFLOW] [INTEGRATION]

#### Business Description
The school often moves stock from one storeroom to another (for example, paper from the Main Store to the Lab Store). A transfer reduces stock in the source storeroom and increases it in the destination by the same quantity, recording both sides as a single linked movement so nothing is lost or double-counted, and notifies the accounts of the internal movement.

#### Actors
- **Initiates:** Store Manager
- **Processes / Approves:** Store Manager
- **Views / Receives notification:** Accountant (via accounting entry)

#### Business Rules
| Rule ID | Business Rule | Rule Type |
|---------|---------------|-----------|
| BR-INV-016 | A transfer creates a matched pair of movements — an outward from the source and an inward to the destination — for the same quantity, and is rejected if the source storeroom does not hold enough stock. | Workflow |

#### Acceptance Criteria
1. A user can move a quantity of an item from one storeroom to another in a single action.
2. After a transfer, the source balance falls and the destination balance rises by the same quantity.
3. A transfer larger than the source balance is rejected.
4. The transfer is reflected to the accounts as an internal stock movement.

#### Integration with Other Modules
- Receives from: None.
- Sends to: Accounting (internal stock-journal entry for the transfer).

#### Enhancement Notes (Future)
- None.

### 3.8 Physical Stock Count & Adjustment
**Requirement ID:** REQ-INV-008
**Priority:** Standard (P1)
**Category Tags:** [WORKFLOW] [APPROVAL] [REPORT]

#### Business Description
Periodically, and at year end, the school physically counts what is actually on the shelves and compares it to the system balance. The Store Manager records the counted quantity for each item; the system calculates the difference (surplus or shortage) automatically. Differences above a configurable value threshold need Principal or Admin approval before the correction is posted, after which the stock balance is adjusted up or down and an audit record is kept. A formatted count sheet can be printed for the government stock register.

#### Actors
- **Initiates:** Store Manager
- **Processes / Approves:** Principal / School Admin (for high-value differences)
- **Views / Receives notification:** Accountant (via accounting entry)

#### Business Rules
| Rule ID | Business Rule | Rule Type |
|---------|---------------|-----------|
| BR-INV-017 | The difference between counted quantity and system quantity is calculated by the system and cannot be entered by hand. | Calculation |
| BR-INV-018 | A stock adjustment whose value exceeds the school-configured threshold must be approved before it is posted. | Approval |
| BR-INV-019 | A surplus is posted as an inward correction and a shortage as an outward correction to the stock balance. | Workflow |

#### Acceptance Criteria
1. A user can start a count, enter physical quantities and see the calculated difference per item.
2. A high-value adjustment cannot be posted until approved; a low-value one posts without approval.
3. Posting a surplus increases the balance and a shortage decreases it, with a ledger entry created.
4. A physical count sheet can be printed/exported for the stock register.

#### Integration with Other Modules
- Receives from: None.
- Sends to: Accounting (adjustment journal entry on posting).

#### Enhancement Notes (Future)
- Mobile barcode-scan counting app (ENH-INV-001).

### 3.13 Purchase Order Management
**Requirement ID:** REQ-INV-013
**Priority:** Core (P0)
**Category Tags:** [DATA_ENTRY] [WORKFLOW] [APPROVAL]

#### Business Description
A purchase order is the formal order placed with a supplier. It can be created by converting approved requisition or quotation lines, or directly for an emergency purchase. The order records the supplier, dates, terms, and for each line the quantity, price, discount and applicable tax, with the line and order totals calculated automatically. Orders above a configurable value need approval before being sent to the supplier, and the order tracks how much has been received so it closes automatically once fully delivered.

#### Actors
- **Initiates:** Store Manager
- **Processes / Approves:** Principal / School Admin (for high-value orders)
- **Views / Receives notification:** Accountant (view), Supplier (the sent order)

#### Business Rules
| Rule ID | Business Rule | Rule Type |
|---------|---------------|-----------|
| BR-INV-031 | Each purchase order is given a unique reference number per year automatically. | Validation |
| BR-INV-032 | A purchase order above the school-configured value threshold must be approved before it can be sent to the supplier. | Approval |
| BR-INV-033 | Each line total is calculated automatically as quantity × unit price, less the line discount, plus the applicable tax. | Calculation |
| BR-INV-034 | A purchase order moves to "received" automatically once every line has been received in full. | Workflow |

#### Acceptance Criteria
1. A user can create an order from approved requisition/quotation lines and as a direct order.
2. A high-value order cannot be sent until approved; a low-value one can be sent without approval.
3. Line and order totals (including discount and tax) are calculated automatically and correctly.
4. The order shows received quantities and closes automatically when fully delivered.

### 3.14 Goods Receipt & Quality Check (GRN)
**Requirement ID:** REQ-INV-014
**Priority:** Core (P0)
**Category Tags:** [DATA_ENTRY] [WORKFLOW] [INTEGRATION]

#### Business Description

## Section 6 — Workflows

### 6.1 Procure-to-Stock Workflow (Requisition → Quotation → Order → Goods Receipt)
**Trigger:** A department needs items not in stock (or a reorder alert).
**End State:** Accepted goods are added to the storeroom balance and recorded in the accounts.

#### Steps
1. Raise requisition: Department Head creates a requisition and submits it.
   - Decision: If approver rejects → requisition is Rejected (with reason); end.
   - Decision: If approved → continue.
2. (Optional) Quotation: Store Manager raises a quotation request from the approved requisition, records supplier quotes and compares them.
3. Create order: Store Manager converts approved requisition/quotation lines (or creates a direct order) to a purchase order.
   - Decision: If order value exceeds threshold → requires approval before sending.
   - Decision: If below threshold → can be sent without approval.
4. Send order: Approved/permitted order is sent to the supplier.
5. Receive goods: Store Manager records a goods receipt against the order, captures received/accepted/rejected quantities, batch and expiry, and runs the quality check.
6. Accept: On acceptance, accepted stock is added to the storeroom, the accounts are updated, the order's received quantity and supplier's last price update, and asset records are created for asset items.
   - Decision: If partially received → order stays Partial; further receipts allowed until fully received, then it closes automatically.

#### Exception Paths
- If a spreadsheet requisition upload has invalid rows: import is blocked and a validation report is shown.
- If received quantity exceeds the outstanding order quantity, or accepted + rejected ≠ received: the receipt is rejected.
- If the goods fail the quality check: the receipt is rejected and no stock is added.
- If the order is cancelled before receipt: order moves to Cancelled and no stock is added.

#### Notifications Triggered
| At Step | Who Receives | Message Summary |
|---------|--------------|-----------------|
| 1 (submit) | Approver | A requisition is awaiting approval. |
| 1 (decision) | Requester | Your requisition was approved / rejected (with reason). |
| 3 (threshold) | Principal / Admin | A high-value order is awaiting approval. |
| 6 (accept) | Accountant | Goods received and recorded; purchase voucher created. |

### 6.2 Stock Issue Workflow (Request → Approve → Issue → Acknowledge)
**Trigger:** A department needs material from the store.
**End State:** Stock is deducted, charged to the department in the accounts, and acknowledged.

#### Steps
1. Raise issue request: Department Head lists items and quantities and submits.
   - Decision: If rejected → Rejected with reason; end.
   - Decision: If approved → continue.
2. Issue stock: Store Manager issues against the approved request (or directly, if authorised), deducting the storeroom balance.
   - Decision: If full quantity unavailable → partial issue; request stays open until fully issued.
3. Record accounting: The consumption is recorded in the accounts.
4. Acknowledge: The receiver acknowledges receipt (mandatory for asset items); an issue slip can be printed.

#### Exception Paths
- If the issue would take the balance below zero: the issue is rejected (negative stock prevented).
- If no approved request exists and the issuer lacks direct-issue permission: the issue is blocked.
- If an asset issue is not acknowledged: it remains pending acknowledgment.

#### Notifications Triggered
| At Step | Who Receives | Message Summary |
|---------|--------------|-----------------|
| 1 (submit) | Store Manager / approver | An issue request is awaiting approval. |
| 1 (decision) | Requester | Your issue request was approved / rejected. |
| 2 (issue) | Receiver | Stock has been issued to you; please acknowledge. |
| 2 (after issue) | Store Manager | Item has fallen to/below reorder level (if applicable). |

### 6.3 Physical Count & Stock Adjustment Workflow
**Trigger:** A periodic or year-end physical stock count.
**End State:** The stock balance is corrected and the variance recorded.

#### Steps
1. Start count: Store Manager creates an adjustment and enters counted quantities per item.
2. Compute variance: The system calculates each difference (counted − system).
3. Submit: The adjustment is submitted.
   - Decision: If total value exceeds threshold → requires Principal/Admin approval.
   - Decision: If below threshold → can be posted directly.
4. Post: On approval/permission, surplus posts an inward correction and shortage an outward correction; the balance updates and the accounts are notified.

#### Exception Paths
- If a high-value adjustment is rejected: it is not posted and the balance is unchanged.
- If the count is abandoned: the adjustment stays in draft and posts nothing.

#### Notifications Triggered
| At Step | Who Receives | Message Summary |
|---------|--------------|-----------------|
| 3 (submit, high value) | Principal / Admin | A stock adjustment is awaiting approval. |
| 4 (post) | Accountant | A stock adjustment has been posted. |

### 6.4 Asset Lifecycle Workflow
**Trigger:** An asset item is received (accepted goods receipt).
**End State:** The asset is tracked through its life until disposal.

#### Steps
1. Create asset: On acceptance of an asset receipt, one tagged asset record is created per unit.
2. Operate: The asset is assigned a location/employee; its condition is updated as needed; transfers are recorded with history; maintenance is logged.
3. Dispose: When retired, the asset is marked disposed and the accounts are notified to write off its remaining value.

#### Exception Paths
- If an asset is under repair: condition is set to "under repair" and it is excluded from active-use views.
- If an asset is lost/damaged before disposal: condition reflects this and disposal records the reason.

#### Notifications Triggered
| At Step | Who Receives | Message Summary |
|---------|--------------|-----------------|
| 2 (maintenance overdue) | Store Manager | Scheduled maintenance for an asset is overdue. |
| 3 (dispose) | Accountant | An asset has been disposed; please write off its value. |

### 6.5 Reorder Automation Workflow
**Trigger:** An item's balance falls to/below its reorder level after an outward movement (or a scheduled check).
**End State:** The Store Manager is alerted and, if configured, a draft requisition exists.

#### Steps
1. Detect: After an outward movement (or scheduled run), the system checks the item's balance against its reorder level.
2. Alert: If at/below the level, the Store Manager is alerted.
3. Auto-draft: If the item is flagged for automatic reordering, a draft requisition for the reorder quantity from the preferred supplier is created.

#### Exception Paths
- If the item is not flagged for auto-reorder: only the alert is raised; no requisition is created.
- If no preferred supplier is set on an auto-reorder item: the alert is raised and the draft is created without a supplier for manual completion.

#### Notifications Triggered
| At Step | Who Receives | Message Summary |
|---------|--------------|-----------------|
| 2 (alert) | Store Manager | Item has reached its reorder level; please replenish. |

### 6.6 Rate Contract Lifecycle Workflow
**Trigger:** A rate contract is created and activated.
**End State:** The contract expires (or is cancelled) and is no longer applied.

#### Steps
1. Create: Store Manager records the supplier, validity dates and item-wise agreed prices.
2. Activate: The contract becomes active and its prices are applied automatically on orders.
3. Monitor: Thirty days before expiry, the Store Manager is alerted to renew.
4. Expire: At the validity end date the contract is treated as expired and is no longer applied.

#### Exception Paths
- If a contract is cancelled early: it is no longer applied from cancellation.
- If two contracts cover the same item: only the active one's price is applied.

#### Notifications Triggered
| At Step | Who Receives | Message Summary |
|---------|--------------|-----------------|
| 3 (pre-expiry) | Store Manager | A rate contract expires in 30 days; please renew. |
