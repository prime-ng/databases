# Reports — Business Requirements

## What This Screen Does

The Reports screen provides cross-module reporting and analytics for the Hostel module. Pre-built reports cover occupancy, attendance, incidents, mess billing, fee collection, and other operational metrics. Reports can be filtered, exported, and printed for management review.

---

## When This Screen Is Used

- Monthly management review of hostel operations
- End-of-year occupancy and financial summary
- Incident trend analysis
- Mess billing audit
- Attendance compliance reports
- Parent/student data requests

---

## Available Reports

**Occupancy Reports**
- Current occupancy by hostel, floor, room type, and gender
- Occupancy trend (monthly/term/yearly)
- Vacancy report (which rooms/beds are free)
- Student roster by hostel/floor/room

**Attendance Reports**
- Daily attendance summary by hostel/shift
- Monthly attendance percentage per student
- Absentee trend analysis
- Late return frequency report

**Incident Reports**
- Incident count by type and severity
- Student-wise incident history
- Warning letter register
- Incident trend (monthly comparison)

**Mess Reports**
- Monthly mess consumption vs billing
- Meal attendance trends
- Special diet requirements summary
- Mess bill register (per student, per hostel)

**Financial Reports**
- Fee demand summary by hostel and type
- Outstanding fee demands
- Revenue collected vs pending
- Damage charge register

**Compliance Reports**
- Visitor log register (for security audit)
- Sick bay admission summary
- Maintenance completion report
- Complaint resolution SLA compliance

---

## Key Features

- All reports have date range, hostel, and other relevant filters
- Reports are exportable to PDF, Excel, and CSV
- Summary charts and graphs for key metrics
- Printable with school letterhead option
- Scheduled report generation and email (optional)
- Role-based data access (wardens see only their hostel)
